Black Friday is a loose theme for a cocktail, so this **maple espresso martini** is designed for an evening get-together after the shopping, rather than a drink to take along. It is a darker, autumn-flavored twist on the [classic espresso martini](/espresso-martini-masterclass/).

![Illustrated maple espresso martini with coffee beans](/images/uploads/black-friday-maple-espresso-martini.svg)

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 oz vodka
- 1 oz coffee liqueur
- 1 oz freshly brewed espresso, cooled
- 1/4 oz maple syrup
- Ice and 3 coffee beans or a small dusting of cocoa for garnish

## How to make it

1. Brew the espresso and let it cool for a few minutes. Chill a coupe or martini glass.
2. Add vodka, coffee liqueur, espresso and maple syrup to a shaker packed with ice. Shake vigorously for 15 seconds.
3. Double-strain into the chilled glass. Garnish with coffee beans or a light cocoa dusting.

Coffee liqueurs differ in sweetness; taste a small sample before adding more maple syrup. For a lower-caffeine evening drink, use decaf espresso. Shake each serving just before pouring for the freshest foam. Keep the prepared espresso refrigerated if making several drinks, and avoid leaving dairy-based additions out at room temperature.
