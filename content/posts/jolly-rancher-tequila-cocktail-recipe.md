## **How to make Jolly Rancher Tequila**

Jolly Rancher Tequila is a tequila flavored with the taste of [Jolly Rancher candies](https://www.hersheyland.com/jolly-rancher). It combines the smoothness of tequila with the fruity and tangy flavors associated with the popular candy brand.

### **Ingredients:**

- 1 bottle of tequila (750 ml) — choose a mid-range blanco or silver tequila for the best flavor combination
- 12-15 Jolly Rancher candies, depending on your flavor preference (I recommend a single flavor for consistency, but feel free to mix and match to create your own unique blend)

### **Instructions:**

1. **Prepare Your Tequila Bottle**: Start by opening your bottle of tequila. If the bottle is full, pour a small amount to make room for the candies. You can enjoy this as a little pre-project shot!
2. **Add the Jolly Ranchers**: Unwrap the Jolly Rancher candies and drop them directly into the tequila bottle. The amount of candy you add depends on how intense you want the flavor to be. I find that 12-15 candies strike the perfect balance between sweetness and the tequila's natural agave flavors.
3. **Shake and Settle**: Once all the candies are in the bottle, secure the lid tightly. Give the bottle a good shake to help the candies start dissolving.
4. **Wait for the Magic to Happen**: Let the bottle sit in a cool, dark place. Shake the bottle a few times a day to help the candies dissolve evenly. Depending on the temperature and shaking frequency, the dissolving process typically takes about 24-48 hours.
5. **Strain (if necessary):** After all the candies have dissolved, check your tequila. If you see any undissolved bits or a cloudy residue, strain the tequila through a fine mesh sieve or cheesecloth into another container to ensure a smooth finish.
6. **Serve and Enjoy:** Once your Jolly Rancher Tequila is ready and clear, you can serve it chilled or incorporate it into various cocktails. A simple mix with soda or tonic water can be delightful, highlighting the candy-infused flavors.

### **Tips for the Best Jolly Rancher Tequila:**

- **Choosing Your Tequila:** Opt for a smooth tequila to enjoy on its own. A cheaper tequila might not mix as pleasantly with the sweet candy flavors.
- **Flavor Combinations:** While single flavors are great, don't hesitate to try combinations like green apple with cherry or watermelon with blue raspberry. Just be sure to balance the flavors to avoid overwhelming sweetness.
- **Serving Suggestions:** This tequila goes great in margaritas or mixed into a tequila sunrise for an extra flavor. Experiment with different cocktails to see how your new creation can substitute regular tequila.
- **Storage:** Your Jolly Rancher Tequila should be stored in a cool, dark place just like regular tequila. It can last for several months if kept properly sealed.

### **What to serve with**

When serving Jolly Rancher Tequila, you have a distinct opportunity to blend the vibrant and fruity essence of the candy-infused spirit with various complementary beverages and dishes.

#### Elegant Snack Pairings

##### 1. Savory:

- **Cheese Board:** Create a cheese board with a mix of sharp, creamy, and pungent cheeses like cheddar, brie, and gorgonzola. Add crackers, nuts, and some honey for dipping.
- **Mini Tacos:** Serve [mini tacos filled with grilled chicken](https://midwestfoodieblog.com/baked-chicken-tacos/), spicy beef, or even flavorful fish like tilapia, topped with fresh salsa and cilantro.

##### 2. Sweet:

- **Fruit Skewers:** Complement the sweetness of the tequila with fruit skewers featuring melon, pineapple, and strawberries.
- **Chocolate Fondue:** Serve a chocolate fondue with marshmallows, pound cake bites, and an assortment of fruits for dipping—the richness of chocolate pairs excellently with the fruity tequila.

##### 3. Spicy:

- **Spicy Shrimp Cocktails**: Spicy shrimp cooked with a hint of garlic and chili, served cool.
- **Jalapeño Poppers:** Stuffed jalapeños wrapped in bacon offer a burst of heat that contrasts pleasingly with the sweet tequila.

## **Ingredients Substitutes**

Making Jolly Rancher Tequila is a fun and creative way to infuse the iconic flavors of Jolly Rancher candies into tequila, creating a unique and flavorful spirit. If you find yourself missing some ingredients or want to explore different variations, there are numerous substitutes you can use to achieve a delightful result.

### **Base Spirit**

- **Tequila:** The primary ingredient in Jolly Rancher Tequila is, of course, tequila. If you're looking for a substitute, consider the following:
- **Vodka:** A neutral spirit like vodka is an excellent alternative. It takes on the candy's flavors well and is a versatile base for various cocktails.
- **White Rum:** Light in flavor, white rum can also pair nicely with the sweetness of Jolly Rancher candies, though it might add a slightly sweeter, more tropical note to the infusion.

### **Flavor (Jolly Rancher Candies)**

- **Jolly Rancher Candies:** These hard candies are crucial for adding flavor and color to the tequila. If unavailable or if you're looking for a different flavor profile, here are some substitutes:
- **Other Hard Candies:** Other fruit-flavored hard candies can be used as a direct substitute. Ensure they are of similar size to maintain the candy-to-liquor ratio.
- **Fruit Syrups:** For a quicker infusion, fruit syrups can mimic the candy's flavor. Note that syrups will significantly sweeten the tequila and alter the texture, making it slightly thicker.
- **Citrus or mint:** Add a strip of lemon or orange zest, or a few fresh mint leaves. Avoid aromatherapy essential oils in drinks; [Poison Control explains why swallowing them can be harmful](https://www.poison.org/articles/essential-oils).

### **Sweetener (Optional)**

If you wish to add a sweetener to enhance the sweetness after infusing your tequila with Jolly Rancher candies, consider:

- **Simple Syrup:** A common sweetener in many cocktails, simple syrup can be easily made by dissolving sugar in an equal part of hot water.
- **Agave Syrup:** For a sweetener that keeps with the tequila theme, agave syrup is an excellent choice, offering a smooth sweetness.
- **Honey or Maple Syrup:** These can add a unique flavor twist in addition to sweetness. They're thicker than simple syrup so that they may change the texture of your tequila slightly.

## **Final Thoughts**

Creating Jolly Rancher Tequila is easy and a fantastic way to impress your friends with a unique, homemade infusion.
