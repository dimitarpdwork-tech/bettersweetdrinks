## **How to make Starbucks Summer Skies Drink**

The Starbucks Summer Skies Drink is a refreshing beverage that's part of their [Starbucks Refreshers®](https://www.starbucks.com/menu/drinks/starbucks-refreshers-beverages) lineup, renowned for its vibrant blue hue and fruity flavor profile. This unique concoction blends the tantalizing flavors of raspberry, blueberry, and blackberry, all shaken with ice and creamy coconut milk.

### **Ingredients:**

- **Water**: The unsung hero. It forms the base of our drink, so let’s keep it nice and pure.
- **Granulated sugar:** Sweetness is key! This trusty pantry staple will balance the tangy flavors.
- **Blueberry extract:** For that deep, fruity undertone that makes you think of sun-kissed berry patches.
- **Raspberry extract:** Adds a bright pop of flavor, like a cheerful burst of summer.
- **Blackberry extract**: Gives a mature twist with its earthy and tart goodness.
- **Citric acid:** This is our secret weapon for that kick, mimicking the zesty vibe of fresh citrus without the pulp.
- **Green coffee bean extract powder:** A nod to Starbucks' roots—it adds a slight caffeinated lift without overwhelming the fruity jubilation.
- **Blue spirulina powder**: Nature’s paint. It transforms the drink into a sky-blue wonder without altering the taste.
- **Raspberry pearls:** These little jewels are the cherry on top, adding a crunchy, fun burst with each sip.

### **Instructions:**

1. **Sweet Syrup Creation:** Kick things off by making a simple syrup. Combine 1 part water to 1 part granulated sugar in a saucepan. Heat until the sugar dissolves. Let this mixture cool off to room temperature.
2. **Flavor Fusion:** To your syrup, add a teaspoon each of blueberry, raspberry, and blackberry extracts. The goal? A harmonious berry melody that’s both refreshing and deep.
3. **Acidity Adjustment:** Stir in a pinch of citric acid. This isn’t just for tang—it helps preserve the vibrant hues of your drink too.
4. **The Caffeine Kick:** Whisk in a scant teaspoon of green coffee bean extract powder. It’s subtle but spirited, giving you a gentle nudge of energy.
5. **Color Me Blue**: Time for the magic! Add a small teaspoon of blue spirulina powder to the mix. Stir well until the color is as vivid as a morning sky.
6. **Chill and Serve:** Pour the mixture into a tall glass over ice, filling it up about halfway.
7. **Top It Off:** Gently add water or sparkling water for a fizzy lift, filling the glass to the top.
8. **Final Flourish:** Sprinkle a spoonful of raspberry pearls over the top. Watch them bob and weave through your creation like little rubies.

### **Pro Tips to Elevate Your Drink:**

- **Chill Your Tools:** Pop those glasses in the fridge a bit before serving. This keeps your drink colder and longer!
- **Experiment with Extracts:** Feel free to adjust the ratios of extracts depending on what berry flavors hit the right note for you.
- **Sparkle and Fizz:** Swap still water with sparkling water to introduce a playful effervescence that tickles the palate.
- **Serving Style:** Serve in a clear glass to show off that mesmerizing blue hue. It’s half the fun!
