A **pomegranate Champagne cocktail** brings color to a New Year's toast without making you run a shaker at midnight. Chill everything first and pour the sparkling wine last. Any dry sparkling wine works; use Champagne if you want Champagne specifically.

![Pomegranate sparkling wine cocktail with lemon twist in a flute](/images/uploads/new-years-pomegranate-champagne-cocktail.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 1 oz chilled pomegranate juice
- 1/4 oz fresh lemon juice
- 1/4 oz simple syrup, optional
- 4 oz chilled dry Champagne or other sparkling wine
- Lemon twist or a teaspoon of pomegranate arils for garnish

## How to make it

1. Put the pomegranate and lemon juices in a chilled flute or coupe. Taste and add syrup if your juice is especially tart.
2. Slowly add sparkling wine; pause if the foam rises. Stir very gently once.
3. Add a lemon twist or a few pomegranate arils and serve immediately.

For a party, combine the juices in advance and refrigerate, then measure about 1 1/4 oz into each glass. Pour the sparkling wine as guests pick up their drinks. For a zero-proof toast, choose nonalcoholic sparkling wine or chilled sparkling water instead. If you prefer citrus, try [Prosecco and orange juice](/prosecco-and-orange-juice-recipe/).
