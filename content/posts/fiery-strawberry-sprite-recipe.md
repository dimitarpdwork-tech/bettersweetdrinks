## **How to make Fiery Strawberry Sprite**

Fiery Strawberry Sprite is a beverage offered by [Burger King](https://www.burger-king.ng/) as part of their "Fiery Menu." It's a unique take on the classic Sprite soda, flavored with strawberry and given a spicy kick with the addition of a fiery strawberry puree.

### **Ingredients You'll Need:**

- **Strawberries:** Fresh is best; about 1 cup sliced will do.
- **Jalapeño**: 1 medium-sized jalapeño, thinly sliced; adjust based on your heat preference.
- **Sprite**: 1 liter, chilled. You can also use any lemon-lime soda for variation.
- **Ice cubes**: For serving.
- **Fresh mint leaves (optional)**: To garnish and add a touch of freshness.
- **Lime:** Squeeze a bit for some added citrus zest and maybe a slice or two for garnish.

### **Instructions to Whip Up Your Fiery Strawberry Sprite:**

#### Prepare the Fruit and Jalapeño:

1. Wash the strawberries and jalapeño thoroughly.
2. Slice the strawberries; they don’t have to be too thin, just enough to let their flavors meld with the drink.
3. Thinly slice the jalapeño. If you’re sensitive to heat, remove the seeds, as that's where most of the heat resides.

#### Muddle:

1. In a large pitcher, combine the sliced strawberries and jalapeño.
2. Use a muddler or the back of a spoon to gently press the strawberries and jalapeño against the side of the pitcher. You want to break them down just enough to release their flavors but not so much that they disintegrate into pulp.

#### Add Sprite and Ice:

1. Fill the pitcher with ice cubes to about half before pouring in the chilled Sprite.
2. Gently stir the mixture to combine. Let it sit for about 5 minutes to allow the flavors to meld together beautifully.

#### Serve:

1. Pour the Fiery Strawberry Sprite into glasses over ice.
2. For an extra fancy touch, garnish with a slice of lime, a few mint leaves, or even a strawberry slice on the rim of the glass.
3. Sip and enjoy the burst of flavors – sweet, spicy, and refreshingly fizzy!

### **Tips for the Perfect Fiery Strawberry Sprite:**

- **Experiment with Infusion Time:** The longer the strawberries and jalapeño sit in the Sprite, the more their flavors will infuse into the drink. If you prefer a milder taste, keep the infusion short.
- **Adjusting the Spice**: Start with a few slices of jalapeño and add more according to your preference. Everyone’s spice tolerance varies, so starting small and adding as you go is better.
- **Chill Your Ingredients:** Make sure your Sprite and fruits are chilled to refresh the drink.
- **Use a Fine Strainer:** If you prefer a clearer drink without bits of fruit or jalapeño, strain the mix before serving.

## **Ingredients Substitutes**

If you want to create a version of the Fiery Strawberry Sprite but need to substitute some ingredients for availability, dietary preferences, or to make a variation, here are some suggestions:

### **Strawberries**

- **Raspberries or cherries:** These fruits can also create a sweet and slightly tart flavor similar to strawberries.
- **Frozen strawberries:** If fresh strawberries aren't available, frozen ones can be a convenient substitute. Just let them thaw before using.

### **Jalapeño**

- **Serrano pepper:** This is a good substitute if you want a similar level of heat. Serranos are typically a bit hotter than jalapeños, so you might use less.
- **Chili flakes or cayenne pepper:** For a simpler approach, a pinch of chili flakes or cayenne pepper can provide the heat without the need for fresh peppers.

### **Sprite**

- **Any lemon-lime soda**: 7-Up, Sierra Mist, or a generic store brand can replace Sprite without significantly altering the flavor.
- **Sparkling water with citrus flavor:** If you’re looking for a less sweet option, you can use lime or lemon-flavored sparkling water. You may need to adjust the sweetness, perhaps by adding a bit of simple syrup or agave nectar.

### **Ice cubes**

- **Frozen fruit cubes:** Instead of regular ice cubes, you can freeze lemon, lime juice, or even small bits of strawberry in ice cube trays. This keeps the drink cold without diluting the flavors as regular ice might do.

### **Fresh mint leaves**

- **Basil or lemon balm:** These herbs can provide a refreshing element if mint is not your favorite or not available.
- **Lemon or lime zest:** For a simpler garnish, you can use citrus zest, which adds an aromatic touch to the drink.

### **Lime**

- **Lemon or orange:** You can easily substitute lime with lemon for a similar acidic and fresh flavor. Orange can add a sweeter, less tart citrus note if that’s what you prefer.
