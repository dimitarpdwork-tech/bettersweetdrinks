This pumpkin milkshake is our own fall-inspired recipe. Chick-fil-A has offered seasonal shakes such as the [Autumn Spice Milkshake](https://www.chick-fil-a.com/customer-support/our-food/our-menu/when-and-where-is-the-autumn-spice-milkshake-available), but that was a different flavor. ## **How to make Chick-fil-A pumpkin Milkshake**

Our version blends vanilla ice cream, pumpkin puree and pumpkin pie spice. It is a homemade pumpkin shake, not a recreation of a regularly offered Chick-fil-A menu item.

### **Ingredients**

#### Milkshake:

- 2 cups vanilla ice cream (preferably a rich, premium brand for creaminess)
- 1/2 cup pure pumpkin puree (not pumpkin pie filling)
- 1/4 cup milk (adjust depending on your desired thickness)
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 3/4 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice (plus a bit extra for garnish)
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar (or to taste)
- Whipped cream for topping

#### Optional for Enhanced Flavor:

- A pinch of cinnamon
- A small amount of nutmeg
- 1-2 ginger snap cookies or graham crackers, crushed (for garnish)

### **Instructions**

1. **Prep Your Ingredients:** Make sure your ice cream is slightly softened for easy blending. Measure out all your ingredients before starting.
2. **Blend the Base:** In a blender, combine the vanilla ice cream, pumpkin puree, milk, vanilla extract, pumpkin pie spice, and brown sugar. Blend on high until the mixture is completely smooth. If the milkshake is too thick for your liking, add a little more milk and blend again. Taste and adjust the sweetness or spice as desired.
3. **Garnish & Serve:** Pour the milkshake into tall glasses. Top each glass with a generous dollop of whipped cream. Sprinkle a pinch of pumpkin pie spice, crushed ginger snap cookies, or graham crackers over the whipped cream for an extra touch of autumn.

### **Tips for Perfecting Your Pumpkin Milkshake**

- **Ice Cream Choices:** The quality of your vanilla ice cream directly impacts your milkshake's creaminess and flavor. Opting for a full-fat, premium vanilla ice cream will yield the best results.
- **Pumpkin Puree Thickness:** If your pumpkin puree is very thick, you might need to adjust the amount of milk to get your desired milkshake consistency.
- **Spice Adjustments:** Feel free to tweak the amount of pumpkin pie spice, depending on how spicy you prefer your milkshake. Adding a pinch of extra cinnamon or nutmeg can enhance the autumnal flavor.
- **Serving Suggestion:** Serve your milkshake immediately after blending for the best texture and temperature. Consider chilling your glasses beforehand to keep the milkshake colder longer.

## **Ingredients Substitutes**

![Pumpkin Milkshake](/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Dutch-Bros-Blue-Drink-5-1024x1024.png)

Making a homemade pumpkin milkshake is a delightful endeavor, especially when you want to customize it or need to substitute ingredients due to dietary restrictions or availability.

### Pumpkin Puree:

- **Substitute**: Sweet potato puree or butternut squash puree. Both offer a similar texture and sweet, earthy flavor compatible with the spice blend typically used in pumpkin-flavored treats.

### **Ice Cream:**

- **Dairy-Free Substitute**: For a vegan version, coconut milk, almond milk, or oat milk ice cream can be used. Choose a vanilla flavor to keep it close to the original taste.
- **Low-Calorie Substitute:** Frozen banana or low-calorie, dairy-free ice cream brands offer a healthier alternative. Note that bananas will add a distinct flavor.

### **Milk:**

- **Dairy-Free Substitute**: Almond milk, soy milk, oat milk, or coconut milk can replace cow's milk without losing the creamy consistency of the milkshake.
- **Low-Fat Substitute:** Skim milk or 1% milk reduces fat content while maintaining creaminess.

### **Pumpkin Pie Spice:**

- **Substitute**: Make your own blend using cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, and cloves. A standard mixture is 3 tablespoons ground cinnamon, 2 teaspoons ground ginger, 2 teaspoons ground nutmeg, 1 ½ teaspoons ground allspice, and 1 ½ teaspoons ground cloves. Adjust according to taste.

### **Whipped Cream:**

- **Dairy-Free Substitute:** Coconut whipped cream or other vegan whipped creams available in markets provide a similar texture and sweetness.
- **Low-Calorie Substitute:** Light whipped cream options or a small dollop of Greek yogurt with a bit of vanilla extract and sweetener mixed in.

### **Vanilla Extract:**

- **Substitute:** If vanilla extract is unavailable, maple syrup or honey can add sweetness and depth. The flavor profile will change slightly but pleasantly complement the other ingredients.

## **Final Thoughts**

Making this homemade pumpkin milkshake with ingredient substitutes allows for customization to suit dietary needs, preferences, and ingredient availability. Experimenting with different replacements broadens the pleasure of cooking and ensures everyone can enjoy this delightful autumn treat.
