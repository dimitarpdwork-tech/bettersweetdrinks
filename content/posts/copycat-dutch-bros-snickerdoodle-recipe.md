## **How to make Dutch Bros Snickerdoodle**

The Dutch Bros Snickerdoodle is a popular coffee drink inspired by the classic cookie. It typically features a combination of espresso, steamed milk, and flavoring that mimics the taste of a Snickerdoodle cookie, often including cinnamon and sugar.

### **Ingredients**

- Espresso or Strong Coffee - 2 shots (about 2 ounces)
- Milk - 1 cup (your choice of milk, with whole or 2% recommended for creaminess)
- White Chocolate Sauce - 2 tablespoons
- Cinnamon Syrup - 1 tablespoon
- Brown Sugar - 1 teaspoon
- Ground Cinnamon - for sprinkling
- Whipped Cream - for topping (optional)
- Cinnamon Stick - for garnish (optional)

### **Instructions**

1. **Prepare the Coffee**: Start by brewing two [shots of espresso](/homemade-stok-espresso-shots-recipe/). If you don't have an espresso machine, you can use 2 ounces of very strong coffee as an alternative.
2. Heat your milk in a saucepan or use a microwave, but be careful not to boil it. Froth the milk if you have a frother, to add that creamy texture to the drink.
3. **Mix the Flavors:** In a serving cup, combine the hot espresso with the white chocolate sauce, cinnamon syrup, and brown sugar. Stir well until all the ingredients are completely dissolved and combined.
4. **Combine the Drink:** Pour the heated, frothed milk into the cup with the espresso mixture. Stir gently to incorporate everything together.
5. **Add the Finishing Touches**: Top the drink with a generous dollop of whipped cream if desired. Sprinkle a dash of ground cinnamon over the whipped cream for that signature Snickerdoodle flavor.
6. **Garnish and Serve:** Place a cinnamon stick in the cup as a garnish. This not only looks attractive but also infuses a bit more cinnamon flavor into the drink as you sip.

### **Tips for the Perfect Dutch Bros Snickerdoodle**

- **Adjust Sweetness:** You can adjust the amount of white chocolate sauce and brown sugar depending on your taste preferences. Some may like it sweeter, while others might enjoy the robust flavor of coffee more prominently.
- **Choice of Milk:** Full-fat milk gives the creamiest result, but you can use any milk including non-dairy alternatives like almond milk or oat milk for a different twist.
- **Homemade Syrups:** If you feel adventurous, make your own cinnamon syrup by simmering water, sugar, and cinnamon sticks until syrupy. This adds a personal touch and can be less sweet than store-bought options.
- **Serving**: Serve this cozy beverage in a clear mug for visual appeal, displaying the layers of cream, coffee, and cinnamon.

## **Ingredients Substitutes**

Making a Dutch Bros Snickerdoodle at home can be a delightful experience, even if you don't have all the original ingredients on hand. Fortunately, many substitutions can yield a drink as comforting and delicious as the original.

### **Coffee or Espresso**

**Substitute:** If you don't have espresso, you can use strong brewed coffee as a base. Instant espresso powder dissolved in hot water can also work if you're in a pinch.

### **Cinnamon Syrup**

**Substitute:** If cinnamon syrup is unavailable, you can make a simple syrup (equal parts water and sugar boiled until the sugar dissolves) and add ground cinnamon or a cinnamon stick while it's simmering. Let it steep, then strain before using.

### **Brown Sugar Cinnamon Syrup**

**Substitute:** For brown sugar cinnamon syrup, replace regular sugar with brown sugar in the simple syrup recipe mentioned above. This will give you the rich, molasses-like flavor that brown sugar is known for, along with the cinnamon.

### **White Chocolate Sauce**

**Substitute:** You can make your own white chocolate sauce by gently melting white chocolate chips with a bit of milk or cream in a saucepan or in the microwave. Stir until smooth. Adjust the milk or cream quantity to achieve the desired consistency.

### **Half and Half**

**Substitute:** If you don't have half and half, you can use a mixture of milk and heavy cream (equal parts) as a replacement. For a lighter option, whole milk can work, or for a non-dairy version, almond milk or oat milk mixed with a splash of a richer, creamier dairy-free milk (like coconut milk) can approximate the texture and creaminess.

### **Whipped Cream (topping)**

**Substitute:** If canned whipped cream isn't an option, you can easily make your own by whipping heavy cream with a bit of sugar and vanilla extract until stiff peaks form. For a dairy-free alternative, chilled coconut cream sweetened with a bit of sugar makes a great substitute when whipped.
