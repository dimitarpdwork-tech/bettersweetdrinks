## **How to make Strawberry Vanilla Sprite**

Strawberry Vanilla Sprite is a variation of [Sprite](https://www.coca-cola.com/ng/en/brands/sprite), a popular lemon-lime-flavored soda that incorporates additional strawberry and vanilla flavors. It typically involves mixing Sprite with strawberry syrup or flavoring and adding a hint of vanilla extract or vanilla syrup to enhance the taste.

### **Ingredients:**

- **Fresh Strawberries**: About a cup, hulled and halved for that burst of juicy fruitiness. For an extra touch of luxury, choose organic strawberries - they're usually more flavorful.
- **Vanilla Extract:** Half a teaspoon will weave in that creamy, dreamy aroma.
- **Sprite:** Choose a chilled bottle or can to add that necessary, invigorating fizz to the drink. If you’re feeling adventurous, a lemon-lime soda works too.
- **Ice Cubes**: Enough to fill your glass to the brim and keep your concoction perfectly chilled.
- Fresh Mint Leaves are optional but recommended for garnish, adding a splash of color and a hint of refreshing flavor.
- **Lime or Lemon Wedges**: Also optional, to garnish and add a tangy twist, if desired.

### **Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the Strawberries**: Begin by rinsing the fresh strawberries under cold water. Gently pat them dry, hull them, and slice each berry in half. If the strawberries are particularly large, consider quartering them for better integration into the drink.
2. **Muddle the Berries**: Place the strawberry halves at the bottom of a sturdy glass. Using a muddler or the back of a spoon, gently press down on the strawberries to release their juices and flavor. You want to break them down a bit but not completely pulverize them—think flavorful chunks rather than a smooth puree.
3. **Add the Vanilla**: Measure half a teaspoon of quality vanilla extract and pour it over the muddled strawberries. The vanilla melds with the strawberry juices, creating a heavenly scent that's pure joy for the senses.
4. **Ice It Up**: Fill the glass with ice cubes, piling them high to ensure your drink stays refreshingly cool.
5. **Pour the Sprite**: Slowly pour the chilled Sprite over the ice, allowing the fizz to cascade and mix with the muddled strawberry-vanilla blend. Pour to the top, leaving a little room if you're adding garnishes.
6. **Garnish (Optional):** Add a sprig of fresh mint to the glass for a fragrant, green flourish. If you like a bit more zest, gently squeeze a lime or lemon wedge over your concoction, then drop it in.
7. **Stir and Serve**: Give the mixture a gentle stir to ensure the vanilla and strawberry flavors are well-distributed throughout the drink. Serve immediately and watch as the first sips draw a chorus of contented sighs.

### **Tips for the Perfect Strawberry Vanilla Sprite:**

- **Use Cold Ingredients**: To maintain the enthusiasm of the Sprite and keep your drink cool, ensure your soda and strawberries are well-chilled before crafting your beverage.
- **Adjust to Taste:** The delightful thing about this recipe is its flexibility. If you prefer a stronger vanilla presence, add an additional dash of extract. For a sweeter touch, choose ripe, in-season strawberries.
- **Serve in Style:** Choose a fun glass—perhaps a mason jar or a tall, slender highball—and consider a striped straw for a whimsical touch.
- **Variations for Every Palate:** If you’re feeling adventurous, experiment with other berry varieties or a combination of fruits to create your signature summer sprite.

## **Ingredients Substitutes**

Creating a Strawberry Vanilla Sprite is an elegant way to enjoy a refreshing, flavored soda. If you’re missing an ingredient or two or if dietary restrictions are a concern, consider these substitutes to ensure your beverage still sparkles with flavor.

### **For Fresh Strawberries:**

- **Frozen Strawberries:** If fresh strawberries aren't in season or available, frozen strawberries are a great alternative. They're picked and frozen at peak ripeness, so they'll still impart a sweet, fruity flavor to your drink. Allow them to thaw slightly before muddling, or use them frozen to chill the drink instead of ice.
- **Strawberry Syrup or Puree:** For a more intense strawberry flavor or if handling whole berries is inconvenient, a tablespoon of strawberry syrup or puree can be used. Adjust the quantity to taste.

### **For Vanilla Extract:**

- **Vanilla Bean Paste:** To elevate the vanilla flavor with a more intense, gourmet touch, use a small amount of vanilla bean paste. Since it’s more concentrated, start with a quarter teaspoon and adjust to taste.
- **Vanilla Sugar:** Rim your glass with vanilla sugar for a subtle aroma and flavor. This won't blend the vanilla throughout the drink as effectively as liquid forms but offers a sweet, fragrant start to each sip.

### **For Sprite:**

- **Club Soda or Sparkling Water**: If you're aiming for a less sweet or lower-calorie option, replacing [Sprite with club soda](/sprite-kool-aid-soda-recipe/) or flavored sparkling water is an excellent choice. Depending on your preference, you might want to add a sweetener to compensate.
- **Ginger Ale:** For a twist with a bit more complexity and a slightly spicy note, ginger ale can be a delightful substitute. This works particularly well if you enjoy a deeper flavor profile or serve the drink as part of a meal.

### **For Ice Cubes:**

- **Frozen Fruit:** For an even fruitier drink, use frozen berries or pieces of lemon and lime instead of regular ice cubes. They’ll keep your drink cold while slowly infusing it with additional flavors.
- **Flavored Ice Cubes**: Consider freezing Sprite or a mix of Sprite and vanilla extract into ice cubes. As they melt, they amplify rather than dilute the drink’s flavors.
