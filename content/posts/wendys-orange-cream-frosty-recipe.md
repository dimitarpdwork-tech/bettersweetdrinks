## **How to make Wendy's Orange Cream Frosty**

Wendy's Orange Cream Frosty is a variation of [Wendy's Frosty dessert](https://www.wendys.com/en-uk/menu-items/large-chocolate-frosty) that blends the flavors of orange and cream. It combines the refreshing taste of orange with the creamy texture of a traditional Frosty.

### **Ingredients:**

- 1 cup milk
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/4 cup corn syrup
- 1/4 cup cream
- 1/4 cup non-fat milk
- Thickeners and additives for color

### **Instructions:**

1. In a blender, combine milk, sugar, corn syrup, cream, and non-fat milk.
2. Blend the mixture on high speed until smooth and well combined.
3. Add thickeners and coloring additives to enhance the visual appeal of the frosty.
4. Blend again until the desired consistency is achieved.
5. Pour the mixture into a chilled glass or bowl.
6. Serve immediately for a delicious and refreshing experience.

### **Tips:**

- Experiment with different thickeners to adjust the texture of the frosty to your liking.
- For a dairy-free option, substitute milk and cream with plant-based alternatives.
- Customize the flavor by adding orange extract or zest for an extra citrusy kick.

### **What to serve with**

When pairing Wendy's Orange Cream Frosty with complementary items, consider options that enhance the overall culinary experience.

- Fresh fruit such as strawberries or pineapple can provide a contrasting tartness that complements the sweet and creamy qualities of the frosty.
- Additionally, light and [crispy cookies](https://handletheheat.com/thin-crispy-chocolate-chip-cookies/) or shortbreads can offer a textural contrast, adding a delightful crunch to each spoonful.
- For a more indulgent pairing, consider serving the frosty alongside a warm slice of pound cake or a fluffy angel food cake to create a harmonious balance of flavors and temperatures.

## **Ingredients Substitutes**

![Wendy's Orange Cream Frosty in a Wendy's cup](/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Poppi-Soda-2-1024x1024.png)

Orange Cream Frosty

As much as we love Wendy's Orange Cream Frosty, sometimes we need to adjust the recipe to cater to dietary restrictions or personal preferences. Luckily, with a bit of creativity, several ingredient substitutes can be utilized without sacrificing taste or texture.

- For those who are lactose intolerant or vegan, milk and cream can be replaced with plant-based alternatives such as almond or coconut milk. Non-fat milk can be replaced with oat milk or soy milk. These substitutions may slightly alter the flavor and consistency of the frosty, but with careful experimentation, a desirable outcome can be achieved.
- For those who are avoiding added sugars, natural sweeteners like honey, maple syrup, or dates can be used instead of sugar and corn syrup. However, it's important to note that these sweeteners may also alter the texture and consistency of the frosty.
- Thickeners and coloring additives can also be substituted for natural alternatives like agar agar or beetroot juice. Care should be taken to maintain appropriate proportions and avoid overloading the frosty with too many of these ingredients.
