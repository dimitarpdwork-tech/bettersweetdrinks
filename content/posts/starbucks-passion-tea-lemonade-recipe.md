## The Secret to the Color & Flavor: It's Not Just Passion Fruit

Here's where the real magic hides. The signature tart flavor and vibrant magenta color don't come from passion fruit. They come from hibiscus flowers! That’s the primary ingredient in the [Teavana Passion Tango™ tea](https://steepster.com/teas/teavana/46836-passion-tango) blend used by Starbucks.

The best and most widely available duplicate for this is **[Tazo Passion® Herbal Tea](https://www.tazo.com/us/en/products/tea-bags/passion/)**. It contains the same key notes of hibiscus, orange peel, and rose hips. That makes it the perfect base for our recipe.

So what does it taste like? It’s bright, tangy, and floral. You’ll get a punch of hibiscus, and a fresh lemonade finish.

## Your Guide to the Simple Ingredients

**The Tea:**Must-Use: 4 **Tazo Passion® Herbal Tea bags**. Brew it concentrated for a bold flavor.

**The Lemonade:**Best Choice for the Starbucks Passion Tea Lemonade: Choose a good quality lemonade that hits that sweet-tart balance. Avoid anything overly sugary or artificial. **[Simply Lemonade](https://www.coca-cola.com/us/en/brands/simply/products/simply-lemonade)** works great.

**Homemade Option:** Prefer to keep things from scratch? Use your favorite homemade lemonade recipe. We have a guide for that: [How to Make Lemonade with Lemon Juice](/how-to-make-lemonade-with-lemon-juice/)[.](/how-to-make-lemonade-with-lemon-juice/)

**The Sweetener:**Authentic Choice: 1/4 cup of **Starbucks Classic Syrup**. This is just a basic simple syrup made from equal parts sugar and water. If you don’t have it ready, here's how to make your own *Quick Simple Sugar Syrup Recipe:* Combine 1/2 cup sugar and 1/2 cup water in a saucepan. Heat just until the sugar dissolves. Cool completely before using.

**The Water and Ice:**Use 2 cups of filtered water for brewing the tea concentrate. It gives the cleanest, freshest flavor. Use Ice to keep it cool and refreshing.

## Essential Tools (And Easy Swaps)

**Cocktail Shaker:**This is the secret to a perfectly chilled, lightly frothy drink, just like they make in-store.

**Don't Have One?**No big deal. A large mason jar with a tight lid works perfectly.

**Other Tools:**You’ll also need a pitcher for chilling the tea and a jigger or measuring cups.

## How to Make Passion Tea Lemonade: Step-by-Step

**Step 1: Brew the Tea Concentrate**Bring 2 cups of filtered water to a boil. Remove from heat. Add 4 **Tazo Passion** tea bags. Let them steep for 5 to 7 minutes. Do not steep longer.

**Step 2: Chill the Tea Completely**Discard the tea bags. Pour the concentrate into a pitcher or jar. Chill in the fridge for at least an hour until fully cold.

**Step 3: Assemble & Shake**Fill your cocktail shaker (or mason jar) with ice. Add equal parts chilled tea and lemonade. Pour in simple syrup to taste.

**Step 4: Shake Vigorously & Serve**Secure the lid and shake hard for about 15 seconds. It should look frosty and a bit foamy. Strain into a tall glass filled with ice.

## **Pro Tips**

- **Make-Ahead Master:** Brew a larger batch of concentrate. Mix with lemonade in a pitcher. Keep chilled. When ready, shake with ice for a fresh glass.
- **Don't Squeeze the Tea Bags:** Avoid squeezing them after steeping. This can release bitterness into your drink.
- **Taste and Adjust:** Everyone likes their drink a little different. Taste and adjust the sweetness to your liking.

## **Fun & Creative Variations**

**Sparkling Passion Tea Lemonade:**Top with a splash of sparkling water after shaking for a fizzy twist.

**Add Fresh Fruit:**Toss in a few muddled raspberries or pineapple chunks for an extra burst of flavor.

**Passion Tea Palmer (Adult Alcohol Version):**Add a shot (1.5 oz) of vodka or white rum before shaking.

**Just the Iced Tea:**Want to skip the lemonade? Mix the tea concentrate with cold water and syrup instead.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

**Is this drink caffeinated?**No. Both **Tazo Passion** and **Teavana Passion Tango** are herbal teas and totally caffeine-free.

**Why doesn’t my drink look as pink as Starbucks’?**This usually means the tea concentrate wasn’t strong enough. Use 4 bags for 2 cups of water.

**Can I use a different passion fruit tea?**You can try, but for the true **Starbucks Passion Tea Lemonade** taste, hibiscus must be the main ingredient.
