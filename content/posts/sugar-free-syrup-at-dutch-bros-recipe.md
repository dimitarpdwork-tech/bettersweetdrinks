## **How to make Dutch Bros sugar free syrup**

Dutch Bros is a popular coffee chain known for its wide array of flavored syrups that customers can add to their drinks. Sugar-free syrups use sweeteners in place of sugar; formulas and nutrition vary by flavor and can change. Check the current label at the store for ingredients and serving information.

![Dutch bros sugar free syrup](/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Untitled-design-2024-05-29T195558.590-1024x1024.png)

### **Ingredients for one vanilla syrup**

- 1 cup water
- A granulated sweetener **labeled as a 1:1 sugar replacement**, in the amount its package recommends for 1 cup sugar. Products vary; concentrated stevia is not a cup-for-cup substitute.
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract labeled for food use, or another culinary extract to taste

### **Instructions**

1. Warm the water in a small saucepan. Add the sweetener according to its label and stir until dissolved; do not boil it down to a thick syrup.
2. Remove from the heat, stir in vanilla, then cool promptly in a clean covered container and refrigerate.
3. Use within 3–4 days. This water-based mixture has not been tested as shelf stable; discard if left out for more than 2 hours. [FoodSafety.gov recommends prompt refrigeration](https://www.foodsafety.gov/blog/10-smart-tips-keep-your-restaurant-leftovers-safe).

**Flavor variations:** Try a food-grade caramel, hazelnut or mint extract, following the manufacturer's suggested amount. Check every flavoring label if you need to avoid added sugar. This is a homemade coffee flavoring, not a copy of Dutch Bros' commercial syrups. If you have dogs, keep any product containing xylitol out of their reach; the [FDA warns that xylitol is toxic to dogs](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/paws-xylitol-its-dangerous-dogs).
