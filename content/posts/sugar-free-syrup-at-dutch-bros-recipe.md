## **How to make Dutch Bros sugar free syrup**

Dutch Bros is a popular coffee chain known for its wide array of flavored syrups that customers can add to their drinks. The sugar-free syrup they offer is typically a sweetened syrup that's made without using sugar. Instead, it's sweetened with artificial sweeteners like sucralose or stevia, allowing customers to enjoy the flavor without the added calories from sugar.

![Dutch bros sugar free syrup](/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Untitled-design-2024-05-29T195558.590-1024x1024.png)

### **Ingredients**

- **White Chocolate Wonderland:** You'll need sugar-free white chocolate flavoring. This elusive gem can be hard to find, but it’s worth the hunt.
- **Classic Chocolate**: A no-brainer, right? Grab some sugar-free chocolate flavoring and let’s get this ball rolling.
- **Captivating Caramel:** Sugar-free caramel flavoring is your golden ticket here. It’s like sunshine in a bottle.
- **Vivacious Vanilla:** The backbone of many a coffee concoction, sugar-free vanilla flavoring stands ready.
- **Chocolate Macadamia Nut Magic:** Combine sugar-free chocolate and macadamia nut flavorings for this dream team.
- **Happy Hazelnut:** Just a dash of sugar-free hazelnut flavoring and you’re golden.
- **Irish Cream Dream:** This one’s a charmer. Sugar-free Irish cream flavoring will do the trick.
- **Cool Coconut:** Coconut flavoring is sugar-free and ready to transport you to the tropics.
- Ravishing Raspberry: Berry up your beverage with some sugar-free raspberry flavoring.
- **Perfect Peach:** Keep it peachy with sugar-free peach flavoring.
- **Sweet Strawberry:** Last but not least, sugar-free strawberry flavoring brings home the berry best.

### **Instructions**

1. **Basic Brew:** Start with the basics. For each syrup, you’ll need 1 cup of water and 1 cup of a granulated sugar substitute. Thanks to the magic of modern food science, options like erythritol, xylitol, or stevia have got you covered.
2. **Mix & Mingle**: In a medium saucepan, whisk together water and your chosen sugar substitute over a medium flame. Keep stirring until they’re fully entwined and the granules have vanished.
3. **Flavor Fiesta:** Once your sweetener has dissolved, here's where the fun begins. Stir in 1 teaspoon of your flavoring choice (more if you’re feeling bold) and a pinch of salt – it’s the secret handshake in the syrup world.
4. **Cool It**: Take it off the heat and let that potion cool down because patience is a virtue, especially when it comes to syrup.

### **Tips & Tricks:**

- **Taste-Test Like a Pro:** Not all sugar substitutes are created equal. Some are sweeter than sweet, others, not so much. Start small with the flavoring, taste, and then add more if you’re craving that extra punch.
- **Storage Smarts:** Bottle up your syrupy creations and pop them in the fridge. They'll keep up to two weeks, but who are we kidding? They’ll be gone before you know it!
- **Layer Up**: Feeling experimental? Mix and match syrups to create your own signature blends. Chocolate raspberry latte, anyone?
- **Keep It Clean:** Cleanliness is next to godliness, especially in syrup-making. Ensure your bottles and pans are squeaky clean to keep things fresh.
