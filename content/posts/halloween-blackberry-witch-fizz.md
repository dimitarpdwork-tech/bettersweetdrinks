This **Halloween blackberry witch fizz** gets its deep purple color from muddled berries. Ginger beer makes it lively and a rosemary sprig gives the glass a little haunted-forest character. The recipe makes one drink; build a tray of glasses by muddling berries and lemon in each, then add the ginger beer just before serving.

![Blackberry witch fizz with rosemary and blackberries in a highball glass](/images/uploads/halloween-blackberry-witch-fizz.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 5 ripe blackberries, plus 2 for garnish
- 2 oz gin
- 3/4 oz fresh lemon juice
- 1/2 oz simple syrup
- 3 oz chilled ginger beer
- Ice and 1 rosemary sprig

## How to make it

1. Muddle five blackberries with the lemon juice and syrup in a shaker. Add gin and ice, then shake for 10 seconds.
2. Double-strain into an ice-filled highball glass if you prefer a clear finish; leave the seeds in for a more rustic look.
3. Top slowly with ginger beer and stir once. Garnish with two blackberries and rosemary.

**Taste before topping up:** blackberries vary in sweetness. Add a little more syrup for tart fruit. Ginger beer is usually nonalcoholic, but check the label if that matters to your guests.

For a mocktail, replace the gin with 2 oz chilled sparkling water and keep the ginger beer. If making drinks for a group, muddle the berries and mix the gin, lemon and syrup ahead; refrigerate and add ice and bubbles to each glass at serving time.
