As the leaves begin to turn and the air grows crisp, there's nothing quite like wrapping your hands around a warm mug of hot chocolate. But what if you're skipping dairy by choice or necessity? Don't worry; you won't have to miss out on this quintessential comfort of cooler months. **Swiss Miss Non-Dairy Hot Chocolate** offers a delightful alternative, ensuring everyone can indulge in the simple pleasure of a hot cocoa moment.

Swiss Miss Non-Dairy Hot Chocolate

## **How to make Swiss Miss Non-Dairy Hot Chocolate**

Swiss Miss Non-Dairy Hot Chocolate is a version of the popular [Swiss Miss](https://www.swissmiss.com/) hot chocolate that doesn't contain any dairy ingredients. It's designed for people who are lactose intolerant or following a vegan diet. Instead of milk powder, it uses non-dairy ingredients like cocoa and vegetable oils to create a creamy texture and rich flavor.

### **Ingredients**

- 1 envelope of Swiss Miss Non-Dairy Hot Cocoa Mix
- 6 oz (approximately 3/4 cup) of hot water or non-dairy milk (almond milk, soy milk, oat milk, etc. for a creamier texture)

#### Optional additions:

- Marshmallows (ensure they are vegan/dairy-free if necessary)
- Whipped coconut cream (for topping)
- A pinch of cinnamon or nutmeg
- A drop of vanilla extract
- A dash of sea salt to enhance the chocolate flavor
- Vegan chocolate shavings or cocoa powder for garnish

### **Instructions**

1. **Heat the Liquid:** Begin by heating your chosen liquid (water or non-dairy milk) until it's hot but not boiling. You can do this in a microwave-safe mug in the microwave or in a small saucepan over medium heat on the stove. If you opt for non-dairy milk, heating it slowly and stirring frequently is key to prevent it from scalding.
2. **Mix in the Cocoa:** Open an envelope of Swiss Miss Non-Dairy Hot Cocoa Mix and pour the contents into your heated liquid. Use a spoon to stir well until the cocoa mix is completely dissolved. This ensures your hot chocolate is smooth and creamy without any lumps.
3. **Add Extras (Optional)**: If you want to elevate your hot cocoa, add extras like a drop of vanilla extract, a pinch of cinnamon or nutmeg, or a dash of sea salt. Mix well to combine the flavors evenly.
4. **Serve**: Pour your hot cocoa into your favorite mug. For an extra festive touch, top with dairy-free whipped cream, a few marshmallows, vegan chocolate shavings, or a light dusting of cocoa powder.
5. **Enjoy**! Wrap your hands around the mug and savor the warmth and delicious taste of your Swiss Miss Non-Dairy Hot Chocolate.

### **Tips for the Perfect Cup**

- **Liquid Choice Matters:** For a richer and creamier hot chocolate, opt for a non-dairy milk option. Each non-dairy milk has a unique flavor and texture, so feel free to experiment to find your favorite.
- **Sweeten to Taste**: If you prefer your hot chocolate sweeter, consider adding a bit of your preferred sweetener. Agave syrup, maple syrup, or even a sugar substitute can be great options.
- **Temperature Control:** Ensure your water or non-dairy milk is hot enough to dissolve the cocoa mix fully. However, avoid boiling, especially when using non-dairy milk, to maintain the best flavor and texture.
- **Customize Your Cup:** Don't hesitate to get creative with your hot chocolate. Adding spices, extracts, or toppings can turn a simple cup of Swiss Miss Non-Dairy Hot Cocoa into a gourmet treat that's uniquely yours.

### **What to serve with**

Swiss Miss Non-Dairy Hot Chocolate can stand alone as a comforting, warm treat, but pairing it with the right companion can transform your cocoa experience into something truly exceptional.

#### 1. Sweet Bites

- **[Vegan Cookies](https://chocolatecoveredkatie.com/vegan-chocolate-chip-cookies-recipe/):** Chocolate chip, gingerbread, or shortbread cookies make a great pairing. Opt for vegan options to keep everything dairy-free.
- **Dairy-Free Pastries:** Look for pastries made without butter or milk, such as croissants, danishes, or sweet bread. A slice of banana bread or pumpkin loaf also pairs wonderfully.

#### 2. Savory Snacks

- **Popcorn:** A bowl of freshly popped popcorn, lightly seasoned with sea salt, makes a surprisingly delightful companion to hot chocolate. The contrast between salty and sweet enhances the flavor of both.
- **[Pretzels](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/easy-homemade-soft-pretzels/)**: Choose plain or salted pretzels for that savory crunch. The saltiness of pretzels combined with the sweet warmth of hot chocolate creates a harmonious taste experience.

#### 3. Fruits

- **Fresh Berries:** Strawberries, raspberries, or blueberries can add a refreshing and slightly tangy contrast to the richness of the hot chocolate.
- **Sliced Apples or Pears:** These fruits, especially when slightly tart, offer a crisp freshness that balances the creamy sweetness of your hot cocoa.

#### 4. Nuts and Seeds

- **Roasted Almonds, Pecans, or Walnuts:** The nutty flavors and crunchy textures beautifully complement the hot chocolate's smoothness.
- **Pumpkin Seeds or Sunflower Seeds:** For a lighter option, these seeds provide a subtle crunch and a nutty flavor.

#### 5. Heartier Companions

- **Vegan Sandwiches or Paninis:** For those seeking something more substantial, a vegan sandwich with grilled vegetables, avocado, and dairy-free cheese can be a satisfying choice.
- **Soup:** A cup of soup, especially tomato or butternut squash soup, can be a warming and comforting side dish on a cold day.

#### 6. Festive Additions

- **Holiday Treats:** Vegan gingerbread men, peppermint sticks, or dairy-free pumpkin pie are festive and fun additions during the holiday season.
- **Homemade S'mores**: Make a campfire classic at home by using vegan marshmallows, a slab of dairy-free chocolate, and a couple of graham crackers.

## **Ingredients Substitutes**

![Non-Dairy Hot Chocolate Swiss Miss](/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Spongebob-Party-Drink-Recipe-Homemade-43-1024x1024.png)

Swiss Miss Non-Dairy Hot Chocolate comes pre-made, designed to be dairy-free from the start, making it suitable for those who follow a vegan diet or have dairy allergies or lactose intolerance. However, if you're preparing your version of hot chocolate at home using a different hot cocoa mix or recipe that calls for dairy, and you'd like to substitute those ingredients to make it dairy-free just like Swiss Miss, here are some ingredient substitutes you can use:

### 1. Milk

The primary dairy component in traditional hot chocolate is milk. Here are some non-dairy alternatives you can use:

- **Almond milk**: Offers a light, nutty flavor and is quite popular in dairy-free recipes.
- **Coconut milk:** Provides a creamier texture and a hint of coconut flavor which can complement the chocolate well.
- **Soy milk:** One of the creamiest milk alternatives, soy milk makes for a rich hot chocolate.
- **Oat milk:** Known for its natural sweetness and full-bodied texture, oat milk is also an environment-friendly option.
- Rice milk: A more watery option with a neutral taste, suitable for those who prefer a less creamy hot chocolate.
- **Cashew milk:** Similar to almond milk but creamier, it can enrich the hot chocolate's texture.

### 2. Chocolate

Many brands of chocolate contain dairy, but there are dairy-free substitutes available:

- **Dairy-free chocolate bars:** Look for chocolate bars that are specifically labeled as dairy-free or vegan.
- **Cocoa powder:** Pure cocoa powder is naturally vegan. Opt for unsweetened cocoa powder and sweeten your drink to taste.
- **Dairy-free chocolate chips:** Use these to melt into the hot chocolate or as a topping.

### 3. Sweeteners

If you need to replace condensed milk or other dairy-based sweeteners:

- **Sugar**: Regular granulated sugar, brown sugar, or coconut sugar work well as straightforward sweeteners.
- **Maple syrup:** This can offer a subtle maple flavor in addition to sweetness.
- **Agave nectar:** A plant-based syrup that is sweeter than sugar and dissolves easily in liquids.
- **Date syrup or paste:** Made from dates, it provides a natural caramel-like sweetness.

### 4. Cream or Heavy Cream

For recipes that call for a creamy topping or a richer texture:

- **Coconut cream:** This is thicker than coconut milk and can work as a whipped topping or stirred in for richness.
- **Soy or Rice Whip:** There are dairy-free whipped creams available made from soy or rice.

## **Final Thoughts**

With these simple steps and tips, you're well on your way to enjoying a cozy, comforting, and completely dairy-free cup of Swiss Miss Non-Dairy Hot Chocolate.
