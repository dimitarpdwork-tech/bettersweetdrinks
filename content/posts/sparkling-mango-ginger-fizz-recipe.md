Mango, ginger and lime make a bright, fizzy mocktail. The ginger syrup contains sugar; adjust the amount if you want a less sweet drink.

![Sparkling Mango Ginger Fizz Recipe garnished with mint leaves and topped with ice, in a cafe in summer afternoon with sun rays kissing the drink, making it vibrant.](/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/sparklingmangogingerfizz.jpg)

### About this drink

Mango puree and lime juice bring fruit flavor, while sparkling water adds fizz. A small amount of homemade ginger syrup supplies sweetness and warmth.

### Why You’ll Love This Drink

- This Mango Ginger Fizz Recipe is non-alcoholic, yet it still feels like a joyful celebration in every sip.
- It is crafted with real fruit and only the freshest, most vibrant ingredients.
- Ginger adds a warm, spicy note.
- Mango adds tropical flavor; the ginger syrup also adds sugar.
- Visually, it is stunning, like holding a glass of golden summer sunset.
- It is ideal for sunny brunches, laid-back picnics, or refreshing alcohol-free happy hours.
- You can adjust the syrup to make it less sweet.

### Ingredients

- 3 oz mango puree or nectar
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 1 oz ginger syrup (see below)
- 4 oz sparkling water or club soda
- Ice
- Fresh mint or basil, for garnish

**To make ginger syrup:** Simmer ½ cup sugar, ½ cup water, and 1–2 inches of sliced fresh ginger. Strain, cool promptly, and refrigerate in a clean covered container. Use within 3–4 days.

### Instructions

1. Start by choosing a tall glass, preferably chilled. Fill it completely with ice to keep your drink refreshing.
2. Measure out and pour in the mango puree, freshly squeezed lime juice, and homemade ginger syrup over the ice.
3. Stir the mixture gently using a long spoon, allowing the flavors to blend without diluting the drink too much.
4. Slowly top off the glass with sparkling water or club soda. Pouring gently helps preserve the bubbles.
5. Finish with a garnish. Add a sprig of fresh mint, a basil leaf, or a thin lime wheel for visual appeal and aroma.
6. Serve the drink immediately while it's cold and fizzy.

### Pro Tips for a great Sparkling Mango Ginger Fizz Recipe

- Use ripe mangoes for a smooth, rich puree.
- Canned mango nectar works well if you’re in a hurry.
- Chill all ingredients before making.
- Make ginger syrup in batches and use within 3–4 days when refrigerated promptly.
- Muddle some mint in the glass for a herbal lift.

### Fun Variations

- **Tropical Pineapple Fizz:** Swap half the mango puree with pineapple juice for a tropical fusion.
- **Spicy Mango Fizz:** Add a thin slice of jalapeño to the glass for a subtle heat kick.
- **Herbal Bliss Fizz:** Muddle fresh basil or rosemary for an earthy, garden-fresh twist.
- **Berry Mango Fizz:** Add a splash of raspberry or strawberry puree for a tangy, colorful upgrade.
- **Coconut Mango Fizz:** Use sparkling coconut water instead of soda for a creamy, beachy vibe.

### What To Pair It With

Anything light, crunchy, and bright will sing with this fizz.

### Frequently Asked Questions:

1. **Is it kid-friendly?**  
   Absolutely. It’s sweet, fruity, and gently spicy. Just go easy on the ginger.
2. **Can I use ginger ale instead of syrup?**  
   Yes, but it will be sweeter. Adjust mango and lime accordingly.
3. **What if I don’t like ginger?**  
   Use less or swap for a splash of herbal syrup. Lavender or rosemary work surprisingly well.
4. **Can I add alcohol?**  
   Definitely. Try vodka, white rum, or gin for a tropical spritz
