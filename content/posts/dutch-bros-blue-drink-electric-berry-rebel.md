If you're on the hunt for a beverage that's as electric in taste as it is in color, look no further than the **Dutch Bros Blue Drink** — a must-try for any adventurous drinker. Known officially as the **'Electric Berry Rebel**,' this vibrant concoction is more than just eye-catching. It's a burst of energy cleverly disguised as your next favorite refreshment.

## How to make Dutch Bros Blue Drink

Dutch Bros Blue Drink is a popular beverage from [Dutch Bros Coffee](https://www.dutchbros.com/), known for its vibrant blue color and refreshing taste. It's a blend of Blue Raspberry and a hint of lime combined with a splash of coconut milk or a coconut-based flavor, giving it a fruity and tropical vibe.

### Ingredients

- **Blue Raspberry Syrup:** This core ingredient gives the drink its unique flavor and iconic blue color. You can find blue raspberry syrup at most grocery stores or online specialty shops.
- **Lime Syrup:** For that refreshing zesty twist, lime syrup is essential. It balances the sweetness of the blue raspberry with a hint of tanginess.
- **Energy Drink:** Dutch Bros uses its brand of energy drink, Rebel, as the base for its Electric Berry Rebel. You can use any energy drink you prefer for your homemade version. A plain, slightly sweet energy drink works best to mimic the original taste closely.
- **Ice**: Needed if you prefer your drink chilled and iced instead of blended.

### Instructions to Make Dutch Bros Blue Drink

1. **Prepare Your Ingredients:** Ensure all your ingredients are within reach. If you're using a blender, make sure it's clean and ready to go. If you're making an iced version, get a tall glass ready.
2. **Mix Your Syrups:** Mix 1 to 2 tablespoons (adjust based on your preference for sweetness) of blue raspberry syrup into the glass or blender. Add 1 tablespoon of lime syrup. If you like your drinks to be tangier, you can adjust the amount of lime syrup accordingly.
3. **Add the Energy Drink**: Pour about 8 ounces (or one cup) of your chosen energy drink over the syrup mixture. If you're blending, add a cup of ice to the blender at this point. For an iced version, simply fill your glass with ice before adding the energy drink.
4. **Mix, Blend, or Stir:** If you're using a blender, blend it well until smooth. For the iced version, stir thoroughly to ensure the syrups and energy drink are well combined.
5. **Garnish and Serve:** For an extra touch of fun, garnish your drink with a slice of lime or some blueberries on top. Enjoy your homemade version of the Dutch Bros Blue Drink!

### Tips for Perfecting Your Dutch Bros Blue Drink

- **Adjust the Sweetness:** Depending on your preference, you might want to start with less syrup and add more as you go. It's easier to make your drink sweeter than it is to balance out an overly sweet drink.
- **Use Good Quality Syrups**: The quality of the syrups can significantly affect the taste of your drink. Opt for high-quality blue raspberry and lime syrups for the best flavor.
- **Chill Your Ingredients:** For the best refreshing experience, especially if making the iced version, chill your energy drink and syrups ahead of time.
- **Experiment with Flavors**: Once you're comfortable with the basic recipe, don't experiment with other syrup flavors to create your custom Dutch Bros-inspired drinks.

### What to serve with

The Dutch Bros Blue Drink, with its sweet and tangy profile, is quite versatile when paired with food or other snacks. When deciding what to serve with this electric blue concoction, consider the time of day and the type of occasion.

#### Morning Kickstart

If you're grabbing a Blue Drink in the morning for a burst of energy, you might want to pair it with breakfast items such as:

- **Bagels**: A classic cream cheese bagel can pair nicely with the drink, with the creaminess balancing out the sweetness of the drink.
- **[Breakfast Sandwiches](https://pinchofyum.com/breakfast-sandwich):** Opt for a savory sandwich with egg, cheese, and your choice of meat for a hearty companion to your tangy beverage.
- **Fruit Bowls:** A bowl of fresh fruit will mirror the refreshing zest of the Blue Drink without overpowering it.

#### Lunchtime Refreshment

During lunch, the Blue Drink can act as a vibrant side to many common lunch foods:

- **Wraps and Sandwiches**: Light turkey or chicken wraps or sandwiches with plenty of greens can complement the drink's fruity notes.
- **Salads:** A crisp salad with a light dressing can contrast the sweetness and provide a refreshing palate.
- **Sushi**: The mild flavors of sushi, especially with a bit of spicy mayonnaise, can be a delightful pairing with the fruity energy drink.

#### Afternoon Snack Pairing

For a quick afternoon pick-me-up, you may enjoy the Blue Drink with:

- **Nuts and Trail Mix**: The salty crunch of nuts can be a satisfying contrast to the sweet and sour energy drink.
- **[Popcorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popcorn):** Whether it's lightly salted or flavored, popcorn is a light snack that won't compete with the flavors of your drink.
- **Cheese and Crackers:** A selection of cheeses with crackers provides a savory balance that can be a great palate cleanser.

#### Sweet Treats

For those with a sweet tooth, pair the Blue Drink with desserts that enhance or balance its sweetness:

- **Vanilla Ice Cream:** The creaminess of ice cream can provide a delightful contrast to the tangy and fruity notes of the drink.
- **Lemon or Berry Sorbet:** If you want to intensify the fruitiness, a tangy sorbet can be a refreshing choice.
- **White Chocolate Treats:** White chocolate macadamia nut cookies, for example, can add a smooth and rich dimension to the experience.

## Ingredients Substitutes

Creating a homemade version of the beloved Dutch Bros Blue Drink, essentially an energy drink-based concoction with a vibrant blue color may require some ingenuity, especially if you're missing a few of the original ingredients. The original is based on the Dutch Bros Blue Rebel Energy Drink, which might not be readily available everywhere.

### Base Energy Drink

**Original**: Dutch Bros uses its Blue Rebel Energy Drink as the base. It's a proprietary energy drink similar to Red Bull but with a unique flavor.

#### Substitutes:

- **Red Bull:** This is the closest in terms of flavor and effect. To mimic the blue color in an adult version, you might need to add some blue food coloring or use Blue Curacao in very small amounts.
- **Monster Energy Zero Ultra:** Slightly sweeter, but it works well as a base. Again, consider adding blue food coloring to achieve the iconic blue hue.

### Flavoring

**Original**: The drink gets its distinct taste from blue raspberry syrup mixed with other undisclosed fruity flavors.

#### Substitutes:

- **Blue Raspberry Syrup:** Available from various brands, it's the perfect match for the fruity sweetness. Torani and Monin offer great options.
- **Homemade Blue Raspberry Syrup**: You can make your own by boiling sugar, water, and fresh or frozen raspberries with a few drops of blue food coloring.
- **Other Berry Syrups:** Any berry-flavored syrup can work in a pinch, though the taste profile may shift slightly. Consider mixing a little lemonade or limeade to add tartness.

### The Distinctive Blue Color

**Original**: The energizing blue color is a significant part of the drink's appeal.

#### Substitutes:

- **Blue Food Coloring:** A drop or two can turn any clear energy drink into a vibrant blue without altering the taste.
- **Blue Curaçao Syrup (Non-Alcoholic)**: This is a great way to add both color and flavor to an adult version.

### Carbonation

**Original**: The original Blue Rebel Energy Drink comes carbonated, adding a refreshing fizz to the beverage.

#### Substitutes:

- **Soda Water:** If your substitute base is not carbonated, adding a splash of soda water can bring in that missing fizz.
- **Carbonated Water Enhancers:** Some brands offer carbonated water enhancers with a berry flavor, which can help replicate both the taste and the carbonation.

## Final Thoughts

Recreating the Dutch Bros Blue Drink at home can be a fun and rewarding experience. Play around with the proportions and ingredients until you find your perfect match, and enjoy the electric flavors whenever you need a vibrant energy boost!
