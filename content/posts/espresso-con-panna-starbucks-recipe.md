## **How to make Starbucks Espresso Con Panna**

Starbucks Espresso Con Panna is a simple yet delicious espresso-based drink. The name "Con Panna" translates from Italian to "with cream." It consists of a shot of espresso topped with a dollop of whipped cream. The whipped cream adds a rich, creamy texture and a touch of sweetness that contrasts nicely with the bold, robust flavor of the espresso.

### **Ingredients:**

- **Espresso:** Ideally, you'll want to use high-quality espresso beans for a rich, authentic flavor. Starbucks uses its own espresso roast, but you can select any high-quality beans you prefer. Depending on your preference, you'll need enough to brew one or two shots.
- **Heavy Cream:** For the whipped cream, opt for heavy cream to achieve that perfectly fluffy and creamy consistency. Approximately ¼ cup should suffice.
- **Sugar (Optional):** If you prefer slightly sweetened whipped cream, add 1 teaspoon of sugar while whipping the cream.

### **Instructions:**

#### 1. Prepare the Espresso

1. Begin by grinding your espresso beans to a fine consistency. If you're using a traditional espresso machine, you'll need about 7-9 grams of ground coffee for a single shot. Double the amount for a double shot.
2. Tamp the grounds firmly into your espresso portafilter. This step is crucial for achieving the ideal pressure and extraction.
3. Brew your espresso directly into a pre-warmed cup. A single shot typically takes about 25-30 seconds of brewing time. If you prefer a more substantial base, opt for a double shot.

#### 2. Whip the Cream

1. Pour the heavy cream (and sugar, if using) into a chilled bowl. Whip the cream using a whisk or an electric mixer until it forms soft peaks. Be cautious not to overwhip; the cream should still be soft enough to dollop on top of the espresso.

#### 3. Assemble Your Espresso Con Panna

1. Gently spoon or pipe the whipped cream on top of the brewed espresso in the cup. The cream should be generous but not overwhelming, allowing both the espresso and cream to share the spotlight.

### **Tips for the Perfect Espresso Con Panna:**

- **Espresso Quality**: This drink's foundation is a robust, well-crafted espresso. Investing in a good espresso machine and high-quality beans will make a significant difference.
- **Fresh Cream:** Use fresh, cold heavy cream for whipping. Cold cream whips better, yielding a lighter, more stable whipped cream.
- **Customization:** Feel free to adjust the amount of whipped cream to suit your taste. Some prefer a dollop, while others enjoy a more generous topping.
- **Serving**: Serve immediately after assembling to enjoy the contrast of temperatures and textures between the hot espresso and the cold whipped cream.

## **Ingredients Substitutes**

![Espresso Con Panna Starbucks Copycat Recipe](/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Spongebob-Party-Drink-Recipe-Homemade-46-1024x1024.png)

Creating a Starbucks Espresso Con Panna at home can be a delicious endeavor. Still, sometimes you might miss some ingredients or need alternatives due to dietary preferences or restrictions.

### **Espresso**

- **Strong Brewed Coffee:** If you do not have an espresso machine, you can substitute the espresso shots with very strongly brewed coffee. Use a dark roast coffee and make it double-strength to mimic the robust flavor of espresso.
- **Instant Espresso Powder:** Another alternative is to use instant espresso powder. It's quick and convenient, and while the taste might not be as nuanced as freshly brewed espresso, it can still provide a satisfactory outcome.

### **Whipped Cream**

- **Coconut Whipped Cream:** For a dairy-free alternative, coconut whipped cream is a fantastic option. Chill a can of full-fat coconut milk overnight, scoop out the solidified coconut cream, and whip it with a sweetener of your choice until light and fluffy.
- **Cashew Cream:** Another plant-based alternative is cashew cream, which is made by blending soaked cashews with water until smooth. Add a touch of vanilla extract and a sweetener, then lightly whip it for a creamy topping.
- **Store-bought Vegan Whipped Creams**: Many ready-made vegan whipped cream options, made from soy, almond, or coconut, are also available, and they can easily replace dairy whipped cream.

### **Sweetener (Optional)**

- **Maple Syrup or Honey:** If you prefer your Espresso Con Panna slightly sweetened, maple syrup or honey are great natural sweeteners that can add a subtle flavor depth. These are perfect for stirring into the espresso before adding the whipped cream.
- **Stevia or Monk Fruit Sweetener:** For a low-calorie option, stevia or monk fruit sweeteners can provide sweetness without the sugar content. These sweeteners are much sweeter than sugar, so use sparingly.
