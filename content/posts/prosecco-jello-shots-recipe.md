## How to make Prosecco Jello Shots

Prosecco Jello Shots is a fun and stylish twist on traditional Jello shots, using Prosecco instead of the typical vodka or other spirits. Prosecco is an Italian sparkling wine known for its light and fruity flavors, which adds a bubbly and sophisticated element to the jiggly, gelatin-based shot.

### **Ingredients**

- 1 cup Prosecco (or any sparkling wine of your choice)
- 1 cup boiling water
- 1/4 cup vodka (optional, for extra kick)
- 2 envelopes (or 2 tablespoons) unflavored gelatin
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- Fresh berries or edible glitter for garnish (optional)

### **Instructions**

1. **Prep Your Ingredients:** Start by gathering all your ingredients. If you are using fresh berries for garnish, wash and slice them as needed.
2. **Dissolve the Gelatin:** In a medium mixing bowl, pour the boiling water over the unflavored gelatin. Stir continuously until the gelatin is completely dissolved.
3. **Add Sugar:** Once the gelatin is fully dissolved, add the sugar and stir until it's completely dissolved as well. This will give your jello shots a touch of sweetness.
4. **Mix in the Alcohol:** Allow the mixture to cool slightly before adding the Prosecco and vodka (if using). Stir gently to combine, ensuring you don't lose too much of the Prosecco's fizz.
5. **Pour into Molds:** Carefully pour the mixture into shot glasses or silicone molds. If using molds, a small spoon or a measuring cup with a spout can make this step easier.
6. **Chill**: Place the filled molds or shot glasses in the refrigerator. Let them chill for at least 2-4 hours or until the jello is fully set.
7. **Garnish**: Once the jello shots are set, garnish each shot with a fresh berry or a sprinkle of edible glitter for a festive touch.

### **Tips for Perfecting Your Prosecco Jello Shots**

- **Texture Tips:** For the best texture, ensure the gelatin is fully dissolved before adding the Prosecco. This will prevent any graininess in your shots.
- **Flavor Variations:** Experiment with different flavors by using flavored gelatin instead of unflavored or by adding a splash of fruit juice. Remember the overall liquid ratio to ensure the jello sets properly.
- **Presentation Ideas:** Serve your Prosecco Jello Shots in elegant shot glasses or fun silicone molds shaped like stars or hearts. For a more sophisticated look, use champagne flutes and garnish with a mint leaf or a twist of citrus peel.
- **Serving Suggestions:** These jello shots are best served chilled. Place them on a tray over ice to keep them cool at a party.
- **Make Ahead:** You can make these jello shots a day in advance. Just cover them with plastic wrap to prevent them from absorbing any odors from the fridge.

## Ingredients Substitutes

![shots](/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Untitled-design-2024-06-25T094600.508-1024x1024.png)

Creating Prosecco Jello Shots is fun and versatile, and you can easily substitute ingredients to suit your preferences or dietary needs.

### **Prosecco Substitutes**

- **Sparkling Wine:** If you don't have Prosecco on hand, any sparkling wine like Champagne, Cava, or even a sparkling Rosé can be used. Each type of sparkling wine will impart its unique flavor profile, so choose one that matches your taste preference. For example, Champagne tends to be drier, while sparkling Rosé will add a fruity and slightly sweeter note to your jello shots.
- **Non-Alcoholic Sparkling Cider:** For a non-alcoholic version, you can use sparkling cider. It will maintain the bubbly texture and add a sweet, apple-flavored twist to your jello shots. This is an excellent option for serving at family-friendly gatherings.
- **Ginger Ale or Lemon-Lime Soda:** Both ginger ale and lemon-lime soda can be used as non-alcoholic substitutes for Prosecco. They will add a fizzy element and a sweet, refreshing flavor to the jello shots. Ginger ale, in particular, can add a subtle spicy note.

### **Vodka Substitutes**

- **Flavored Vodka:** For an extra layer of flavor, substitute regular vodka with flavored vodka such as citrus, berry, or even vanilla. This will enhance the overall taste and complement the Prosecco's fruity notes.
- **White Rum:** White rum can be a good alternative to vodka, adding a slight sweetness and a hint of tropical flavor to your jello shots. This substitution works exceptionally well if you are using a sparkling wine with a fruity profile.
- **Non-Alcoholic Options:** If you prefer a non-alcoholic version, you can simply omit the vodka or replace it with an equal amount of fruit juice, such as white grape juice or apple juice. This will maintain the liquid ratio needed for the jello to set properly.

### **Unflavored Gelatin Substitutes**

- **Flavored Gelatin:** If you can't find unflavored gelatin, flavored gelatin (like Jell-O) can be used. Choose a flavor that pairs well with Prosecco, such as lemon, strawberry, or raspberry. This will add both color and taste to your jello shots. Just remember to adjust the sugar content accordingly, as flavored gelatin is already sweetened.
- **Agar-Agar**: For a vegetarian or vegan option, agar-agar is a great substitute. It's made from seaweed and works similarly to gelatin. You can use it in a 1:1 ratio with gelatin. Keep in mind that agar-agar sets firmer than gelatin, so the texture of your jello shots will be slightly different.
- **Pectin:** Another vegetarian alternative is pectin, which is derived from fruits. Pectin can be used to make jello shots, but it will result in a different texture that is more jelly-like and less wobbly than gelatin. Use it according to the package instructions for best results.

### **Sugar Substitutes**

- **Honey or Agave Syrup:** Both honey and agave syrup can be used as natural sweeteners in place of sugar. They will add a subtle flavor of their own—honey adds a floral note, while agave syrup adds a mild sweetness. Use them in a 1:1 ratio.
- **Stevia or Erythritol:** For a low-calorie or keto-friendly option, stevia or erythritol can be used as sugar substitutes. Both are much sweeter than sugar, so you will need to adjust the amount accordingly. Typically, you'll use about 1/4 to 1/2 the amount of stevia or erythritol compared to sugar.
- **Maple Syrup:** Maple syrup can also be used as a sweetener. It will add a rich, caramel-like flavor to your jello shots. Substitute it in a 1:1 ratio for sugar, but be mindful that it may slightly change the color of your jello shots.
