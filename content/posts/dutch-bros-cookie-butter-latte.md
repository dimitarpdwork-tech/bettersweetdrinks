## **How to make Dutch Bros Cookie Butter Latte**

The Dutch Bros Cookie Butter Latte is a popular drink from [Dutch Bros Coffee](https://www.dutchbros.com/), a coffee chain known for its various flavored beverages. This particular latte is inspired by the flavors of cookie butter, a sweet, spiced spread made from crushed cookies, typically speculoos.

### **Ingredients:**

- 2 shots of espresso or about 1/2 cup of strong brewed coffee
- 1 tablespoon of cookie butter spread (Speculoos/Biscoff or similar)
- 1/2 cup of milk (feel free to use the milk of your choice, such as whole, skim, almond, or oat milk)
- Optional sweetener to taste (sugar, honey, or preferred alternative)
- Whipped cream or a frothy milk topping (for the Dutch Bros Soft Top™ effect)
- Cookie butter and/or crushed Speculoos/Biscoff cookies for garnish
- Cinnamon or nutmeg (optional for additional spicing)

### **Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the Coffee Base:** Begin by brewing your espresso or strong coffee. Aim for a more concentrated brew that resembles espresso strength if you're using a coffee maker or French press.
2. **Heat and Froth the Milk**: While your coffee is brewing, heat your milk. You can do this on the stove or in the microwave until it's warm but not boiling. Be careful not to scorch the milk. If you have a milk frother, froth the milk until it's creamy and has doubled in volume. If you don't have a frother, you can shake the warmed milk vigorously in a jar or use a whisk.
3. **Mix in the Cookie Butter**: In your coffee cup, combine the freshly brewed espresso or coffee with the cookie butter. Stir until the cookie butter has fully dissolved into the coffee. Taste and add your preferred sweetener if you feel the drink needs more sweetness.
4. **Combine and Top:** Pour the frothed milk over the coffee and cookie butter mixture. For a professional touch, hold back the foam with a spoon. Let the liquid milk pour in first, then top with foam.
5. **Garnish**: For an authentic Dutch Bros experience, top your latte with a whipped cream swirl. Drizzle with a bit of cookie butter or sprinkle with crushed cookies. If you like, a dusting of cinnamon or nutmeg can add an extra layer of spice.

### **Tips for Making the Perfect **Dutch Bros**** **Cookie Butter Latte:**

- **Quality Coffee:** Use freshly brewed high-quality coffee or espresso for the best flavor.
- **Milk Texture:** Properly frothed milk should be creamy and not too airy. Gentle heating and frothing prevent the milk from being too bubbly.
- **Dissolving Cookie Butter:** Ensure the cookie butter is at room temperature so it dissolves easily into the coffee.
- **Balance the Sweetness**: Cookie butter and toppings can make the latte quite sweet, so adjust the amount of added sweetener to your preference.
- **Garnishes**: Get creative with garnishes, perhaps adding a caramel drizzle, chocolate shavings, or a dusting of powdered sugar for a little extra indulgence.
- **Dairy-Free Options**: For a vegan-friendly latte, substitute regular milk with almond, soy, or oat milk and use dairy-free whipped cream.

## **Ingredients Substitutes**

![ Cookie Butter Latte Homemade Recipe](/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Add-a-heading-16-1024x1024.png)

Creating a Dutch Bros Cookie Butter Latte at home can be a delightful adventure, especially when you learn to play around with ingredient substitutes to cater to your taste and dietary preferences or simply work with what you have. Here's how to substitute the main ingredients without compromising the essence of this delicious beverage.

### **Cookie Butter**

- **Homemade Cookie Butter:** You can make your version using crushed Biscoff cookies or graham crackers mixed with butter and sweetened condensed milk to get the right spreadable consistency.
- **Nut Butter:** For a nuttier flavor profile, consider almond or cashew butter. These won't replicate the spiced cookie taste but will add a creamy, nutty layer.
- **Pumpkin Butter**: For a fall-themed latte, pumpkin butter is an excellent substitute. This provides a different but equally delightful seasonal spice profile.

### **Espresso**

- **Strong-Brewed Coffee:** If you don't have an espresso machine, use a strong-brewed coffee substitute. A French press or a dark-roasted drip coffee can work well.
- **Instant Espresso Powder**: Another quick alternative is instant espresso powder. It might not offer the depth of freshly brewed espresso, but it can be convenient and still tasty.

### **Milk**

- **Dairy-Free Alternatives:** For a vegan or lactose-free option, almond milk, oat milk, or coconut milk can be excellent substitutes. Each will add a unique flavor note to the latte. Oat milk, for example, tends to be creamy and slightly sweet, complementing the cookie butter flavor well.
- **Half and Half:** You might use half and half instead of whole milk for a creamier texture.

### **Sweeteners**

- **Maple Syrup or Honey:** If you're avoiding refined sugars or simply want to experiment with different sweet profiles, maple syrup or honey can be great natural sweeteners. Adjust quantities to taste, as these can be sweeter than regular sugar.
- **Brown Sugar or Coconut Sugar:** Brown sugar will add a richer, more caramel-like sweetness, enhancing the cookie butter flavor, while coconut sugar offers a lower-glycemic index alternative with a slight caramel note.

### **Vanilla Extract**

- **Vanilla Bean Paste:** If you're looking for an upgrade or a more intense vanilla flavor, vanilla bean paste can be a superior substitute for vanilla extract. A little goes a long way!
- **Almond Extract:** For a slightly different flavor profile, almond extract can add a nutty aroma, complementing the latte's creamy and spiced notes.

### **Whipped Cream (Optional)**

- **Dairy-Free Whipped Cream:** Coconut whipped cream or any plant-based whipped topping can be used for those who avoid dairy but still want that luxurious finish.
- **Homemade Whipped Cream:** If you prefer controlling the amount of sugar, homemade whipped cream is easy to make with heavy cream and a sweetener of your choice.

### **Garnishes (Optional)**

- **Spices**: A sprinkle of cinnamon, nutmeg, or even pumpkin spice can substitute for crushed cookie garnish for added aroma without the crunch.
- **Chocolate Shavings:** Garnish with chocolate shavings or cocoa powder instead of cookie crumbs for a chocolatey twist.

## **Final Thoughts**

Now that you have all the steps and tips, it's time to whisk up your Dutch Bros Cookie Butter Latte and revel in its exquisite flavors from the comfort of your home. Enjoy the aromatic pleasure as it fills your home and senses, reminding you of the simple joys of fall flavors.
