## **How to make Smoothie King Watermelon Xtreme Drink**

Smoothie King Watermelon Xtreme is a smoothie blend typically featuring watermelon as the primary flavor. It's designed to be a refreshing and hydrating option, often including other fruits and ingredients to enhance the taste and nutritional profile.

### **Ingredients:**

- **Two cups of fresh watermelon juice**: To prepare, blend fresh watermelon cubes in a blender and strain to remove the pulp, achieving a smooth juice. If in a rush, buying pre-made watermelon juice is also an option; just be mindful of added sugars.
- **1 cup of frozen strawberries**: Strawberries add a tangy, sweet depth, and freezing them beforehand enhances the smoothie's creamy texture.
- **1/2 cup of coconut water**: This adds a tropical hint and contributes to the hydration factor, making it perfect for a post-workout refreshment.
- **1/2 cup of dairy whey blend:** To recreate the protein component commonly found in Smoothie King drinks, you can use a high-quality, plain or vanilla-flavored whey protein powder. If you prefer a non-dairy option, pea protein or any other plant-based protein powder are good substitutes.

### **Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the Watermelon Juice:** If starting from scratch, chop your watermelon into cubes, discarding any seeds. Blend until smooth, then strain using a fine mesh sieve to extract the juice. Measure out 2 cups of juice for the smoothie.
2. **Combine Ingredients:** In a blender, add the 2 cups of watermelon juice, frozen strawberries, coconut water, and dairy whey blend (or protein powder of your choice).
3. **Blend Until Smooth:** Cover and blend quickly until all the ingredients are thoroughly mixed and the texture is smooth. If the consistency is too thick, you can add a bit more coconut water to thin it out.
4. **Taste and Adjust**: If the smoothie isn't sweet enough, you can add a bit of honey or agave syrup. Likewise, if it's too sweet, a splash of more coconut water or a squeeze of lime can help balance it out.
5. **Serve Immediately:** Pour the smoothie into your preferred glass and, if desired, garnish with a strawberry slice or a small wedge of watermelon.

### **Tips:**

- **Opt for Quality Ingredients:** Since this smoothie has a relatively simple ingredient list, the quality of each component really shines through. Opt for ripe, flavorful strawberries and fresh watermelon for the best taste.
- **Strawberry Swap:** If strawberries are out of season or not to your preference, raspberries can make a good substitute, offering a similar color and tartness.
- **Hydration Boost:** If you're making this smoothie as a post-exercise drink, the coconut water is excellent for electrolyte replenishment. To further this benefit, adding a pinch of salt can help with sodium replenishment.
- **Whey Protein Alternatives:** For those with dairy sensitivities or preferences, switching the dairy whey blend for a plant-based protein can make this smoothie vegan-friendly. This might slightly alter the taste.

### **What to serve with**

Pairing something with the Smoothie King Watermelon Xtreme drink creates a delightful dining experience, especially for occasions like a refreshing summer brunch, a post-workout meal, or a light afternoon treat. Considering the drink's refreshing and slightly sweet profile, here are some detailed suggestions on what to serve with it to enhance your culinary experience:

#### For Breakfast or Brunch:

- **[Egg White Veggie Omelets](https://www.eatingbirdfood.com/egg-white-omelette/):** A fluffy, protein-rich egg white omelet filled with your choice of vegetables like spinach, tomatoes, and bell peppers offers a light yet satisfying complement to the watermelon smoothie. This combination ensures a balanced protein intake, healthy fats, vitamins, and minerals.
- **Whole Grain Toast with Avocado**: For a heart-healthy option, whole grain toast topped with mashed avocado, a sprinkle of salt, pepper, and a squeeze of lemon pairs wonderfully with the sweet and tangy notes of the smoothie.

#### For a Post-Workout Meal:

- **Grilled Chicken Salad:** Lean protein replenishes muscle after a workout, and a salad with grilled chicken breast, mixed greens, cucumber, and a light vinaigrette is a lean, protein-packed choice that goes well with the hydrating smoothie.
- **[Quinoa and Black Bean Bowl](https://toriavey.com/quinoa-black-bean-burrito-bowls/):** For a plant-based protein boost, a quinoa bowl with black beans, corn, diced tomatoes, and avocado, seasoned with lime and cilantro, can complement the smoothie while providing essential amino acids and fiber.

#### For an Afternoon Snack or Light Meal:

- **Nut and Seed Bars:** Homemade or store-bought bars packed with nuts, seeds, and a touch of honey offer a crunchy texture and a nutritious snack that pairs excellently with the refreshing qualities of a watermelon smoothie.
- **Fruit and Cheese Platter:** A platter with sliced apples, grapes, and chunks of pineapple, alongside a selection of cheeses like feta, mozzarella, or gouda, provides a delightful balance of sweet and savory to accompany the smoothie.

#### For a Sweet Treat:

- **Yogurt Parfaits:** Layered Greek yogurt parfaits with granola and fresh berries offer a creamy texture and a pleasant sweetness that beautifully complements the watermelon smoothie without being overly indulgent.
- **Angel Food Cake:** A slice of light, fluffy angel food cake, perhaps with a dollop of whipped cream and fresh strawberries, creates a dreamy, light dessert pairing that echoes the smoothie's fresh flavors without competing.

## **Ingredients Substitutes**

![Watermelon Xtreme Drink](/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Add-a-heading-13-1024x1024.png)

The Smoothie King Watermelon Xtreme is a refreshing summertime favorite that features the sweet and hydrating qualities of watermelon. If you're looking to make a similar smoothie at home and need to substitute ingredients due to availability or personal preferences, here are some detailed alternative options:

### **Watermelon Juice Blend:**

- **Homemade Watermelon Juice:** Puree fresh watermelon chunks in a blender and strain to make homemade juice.
- **Other Melon Juices:** Cantaloupe or honeydew juice can provide a similar sweetness and hydrating effect.
- **Coconut Water:** Using coconut water as a base liquid for a tropical twist with added electrolytes.

### **Watermelon:**

- **Frozen Watermelon:** If fresh watermelon is unavailable, frozen watermelon will do. It also chills the smoothie without dilution from ice.
- **Mixed Berries:** A combination of strawberries, raspberries, and blueberries can offer a different but tantalizing flavor profile.

### **Turbinado Sugar:**

- **Honey or Agave Syrup:** Both are natural sweeteners that can give the smoothie a more complex sweetness.
- **Maple Syrup:** Adds sweetness with a hint of maple flavor, which can create an interesting taste combination.
- **Stevia or Monk Fruit Sweetener:** Use a natural sugar substitute like stevia or monk fruit sweetener for a lower-calorie alternative.

### **Electrolyte Blend:**

- **DIY Electrolyte Mix:** Combine a small pinch of salt (for sodium) with a teaspoon of natural sweetener and a mix of potassium-rich fruit juices like orange or banana.
- **Sports Drinks:** Use a low-sugar sports drink to add electrolytes without significantly altering the taste.
- **Electrolyte Powders or Tablets:** To boost the electrolyte content of your smoothie, dissolve these in water first.
