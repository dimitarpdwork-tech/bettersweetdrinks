## How to make Homemade Prime Hydration Drink

This homemade drink takes inspiration from flavored hydration beverages, but its mineral content depends on your ingredients and it is not the branded formula or a measured oral rehydration solution. For everyday hydration, water and regular meals are generally enough.

### Ingredients

- **Coconut Water (2 cups):** Coconut water adds a lightly sweet flavor; check the label for added sugar.
- **Honey (1-2 tablespoons):** Honey adds sweetness; use less or omit it if preferred.
- **Sea Salt (1/8 teaspoon)**: A pinch of sea salt adds flavor and some sodium; it is not a measured electrolyte dose.
- **Lemon Juice (2 tablespoons):** This citrus superstar is high in vitamin C and provides a refreshing zest to the drink.
- **Water (2 cups):** Water is the cornerstone of hydration and will help dilute the coconut water and balance the drink's flavor.
- **Ice (Optional):** If you prefer your drink chilled.

#### Optional flavor additions:

- **[Fruit puree](/passion-fruit-puree-recipe/) or juice:** Add a little for flavor. This recipe is not a substitute for a formulated oral rehydration solution.

### Instructions

Now, let's put together your homemade Prime Hydration Drink. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. **Blend the Bases:** Start by pouring the coconut water and plain water into a large pitcher or jug. The coconut water should provide enough sweetness and flavor to carry the drink, but the water helps ensure it's not overpoweringly sweet or too rich.
2. **Sweeten It Up:** Stir in the honey until thoroughly dissolved. You can adjust the amount based on your desired level of sweetness. If you're opting for a sugar-free version, skip the honey or use a sweetener like stevia.
3. **Add the Zest:** Squeeze in your fresh lemon juice for that zingy kick. The acidity should balance nicely against the sweetness of the honey and the coconut water.
4. **Salt It Right:** Sprinkle in the sea salt. The pinch of salt adds flavor and some sodium; it is not a precise electrolyte dose.
5. **Chill and Serve:** If you're using ice, now is the time to add it to your pitcher. Serve your hydration drink chilled for immediate refreshment or store it in the fridge for later.

### Flavor and serving tips

- **Fresh is Best:** Use fresh lemon juice rather than bottled variants to maximize the flavor and nutritional profile.
- **Balance Your Sweetness:** Adjust the level of honey depending on your sweetness preference and nutritional goals.
- **Experiment with Flavors:** Don't be shy about adding other fruit purees or juices to mix up the flavor profile. Creating your version of a tropical or berry mix can be exciting!
- **Flavor first:** Fresh lemon and a clean pitcher make the biggest difference to the taste.
- **For everyday use:** Enjoy it if you like the flavor; you do not need this drink every day to stay hydrated.

### What to serve with

Serving your homemade Prime Hydration Drink is all about balancing flavors and choosing complementary foods to enhance the overall experience. Whether you're enjoying the drink post-exercise, as a daily refreshment, or at a social event, here's what can go well with your Prime Hydration Drink:

#### Post-Workout Snacks

After a good workout, you want to replenish your body with nutrients that aid recovery:

- **[Protein-Rich Snacks](https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/healthy-high-protein-snacks):** Consider pairing your hydration drink with a small portion of Greek yogurt, a protein bar, or a handful of almonds. The protein will help repair and build muscles, while the drink provides a refreshing accompaniment.
- **Fresh Fruit:** Choose fruits like bananas or apples, which provide carbohydrates for energy replenishment, along with additional hydration from their natural water content.
- **Whole Grain Toast:** Topped with your favorite nut butter or avocado, whole grain toast adds complex carbs and healthy fats, making for a balanced snack.

#### Daily Hydration

If you're sipping your hydration drink throughout the day, consider light and healthy pairings:

- **Vegetable Sticks and Hummus:** Crunchy vegetables such as carrots or cucumber along with hummus are a delightful and nutritious choice that won't weigh you down.
- **Nuts and Seeds:** A small serving of mixed nuts or seeds can give you a boost of healthy fats, fiber, and protein.
- **[Low-Fat Cheese](https://www.verywellhealth.com/which-cheeses-can-i-eat-on-a-cholesterol-lowering-diet-697777)**: Cheese strings or cubes of low-fat cheese can provide calcium and protein without overpowering the refreshing taste of your drink.

#### Social Gatherings or Events

For social occasions where your Prime Hydration Drink is being served as a key beverage:

- **Finger Foods:** Serve the drink with an assortment of finger foods like veggie platters, cheese and crackers, or canapés that guests can enjoy casually.
- **Light Salads:** A crisp garden or fruit salad complements the drink's freshness without overshadowing its flavor.
- **Grilled Seafood or Chicken:** If serving a meal, opt for lighter grilled proteins like seafood or chicken, which won't weigh down your palate.

#### Summer Barbecues or Picnics

During outdoor gatherings, your chilled hydration drink is incredibly refreshing:

- **Grilled Vegetables:** Grilled zucchini, bell peppers, and mushrooms can provide a savory contrast to the sweetness of the drink.
- **Kabobs:** Whether it's fruit kabobs for a sweet touch or chicken and vegetable kabobs for something more savory, they're perfect for outdoor sipping and snacking.
- **Cold Pasta Salads:** A chilled pasta salad with lots of fresh veggies and a light vinaigrette can be a hit when served alongside your homemade hydration concoction.

## Ingredients Substitutes

![Hydration Drink](/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Prime_Hydration_Flavours-1024x423.jpg)

Creating a homemade Prime Hydration Drink provides flexibility in terms of ingredients, allowing you to customize the beverage to suit your dietary needs, taste preferences, and availability of ingredients. Here are detailed substitutes for the key components:

### Coconut Water

#### **Substitutes**:

- **Electrolyte Water:** If coconut water is difficult to come by, electrolyte-infused water can be an excellent alternative, as it also supplies the necessary minerals for hydration.
- **Cucumber Water:** For a low-calorie option, blend cucumber slices with water. Cucumbers have a high water content and can add a refreshing taste.
- **Rice Milk:** If you're after the slightly sweet taste of coconut water, rice milk can be a good substitute, although it's lower in electrolytes.

### Honey

#### Substitutes:

- **Maple Syrup**: Offers a similar sweetness with a maple flavor.
- **[Agave Nectar](https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/agave-nectar-is-even-worse-than-sugar):** A vegan substitute that's sweeter than honey, meaning you may need less to achieve the same sweetness level.
- **Stevia:** For a sugar-free option, stevia can sweeten your drink without adding calories. Adjust to taste since it's much sweeter than honey.

### Sea Salt

#### Substitutes:

- **Pink Himalayan Salt:** Use the same small quantity for a slightly different flavor.
- **Table Salt:** Use the same small quantity; it also adds sodium and flavor.

### Lemon Juice

#### Substitutes:

- **Lime Juice**: Offers a similar citrus profile and vitamin C content, providing that refreshing zing to the drink.
- **Orange Juice:** For a sweeter, less tart citrus flavor, orange juice can be used. It also boosts the drink's vitamin C content.
- **White Vinegar:** In very small quantities, white vinegar can somewhat mimic the acidity of lemon juice, though it lacks the citrus flavor.

### Water

#### Substitutes:

- **Sparkling Water:** If you prefer a fizzy drink, sparkling water can replace still water for an effervescent hydration drink.
- **Herbal Teas:** Brewed and chilled herbal teas, such as peppermint or chamomile, can add a flavorful and therapeutic twist to your hydration drink.

## Final Thoughts

Now that you have the ingredients, instructions, and tips in hand, making your homemade Prime Hydration Drink is as simple as it gets. Enjoy the flavor and adjust the sweetness to taste.
