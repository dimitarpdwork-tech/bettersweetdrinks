## **How to make the Summer Berry Lemonade** Refresher at home

The Summer Berry Lemonade Refresher, as the name suggests, is a refreshing beverage typically available during the summer months at [Starbucks](https://www.starbucks.com/) locations. It's made with a blend of lemonade, mixed berry juice, and ice, creating a sweet and tangy flavor profile with hints of berries and citrus. This drink is often popular for its thirst-quenching properties and vibrant taste, making it a favorite choice for customers seeking a cool and fruity beverage during the warmer seasons.

### **Ingredients Needed:**

- **Lemonade:** We're going simple with the base—just your run-of-the-mill, store-bought lemonade will do! Of course, if you’re feeling fancy, squeezing a few fresh lemons could only make it better.
- **Mixed Berry Syrup:** You can find this at most grocery stores, but for those who love a DIY challenge, I'll drop a quick recipe below.
- **Fresh Berries:** Grab some raspberries, blackberries, and blueberries for that authentic Starbucks burst of berry goodness.
- **Ice**: Because what’s a cold summertime drink without it?

#### Homemade Mixed Berry Syrup (Optional):

- 1 cup Mixed Berries (the more the merrier)
- 1 cup Sugar (no sugar, no fun)
- 1 cup Water (keep it balanced)

### **Instructions to make the Summer Berry Lemonade Refresher at home:**

1. Combine the Berries, Sugar, and Water in a saucepan over medium heat.
2. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and let it simmer. Keep it going until the sauce thickens slightly—about 10 minutes should do the trick.
3. Strain it. Unless you’re into a chunky texture, it’s best to get those berry bits out. Just pour the mixture through a fine mesh sieve, and voila, silky smooth berry syrup!

#### Lemonade Assembly:

1. Fill a glass with ice — Don't be shy; pack it in!
2. Pour over the lemonade, filling the glass to about two-thirds. Room has to be made for that berrylicious magic, after all.
3. Stir in about one to two tablespoons of your mixed berry syrup, depending on how sweet you like your life.
4. Toss in a handful of fresh berries. They’re not just there for looks; they add a burst of freshness with every sip!
5. Mix it up! Give your concoction a good stir with a long spoon or a straw.

### **Tips for the **Summer Berry Lemonade** Refresher Recipe Perfection:**

- **Chill your lemonade and syrup beforehand:** This keeps everything icy cool and minimizes the melting of your ice.
- **Adjust sweetness to taste**: Not everyone’s sweet tooth is created equal, so start with less syrup and add as you go.
- **Serve immediately:** Freshness is your best friend here, so mix these beauties up and serve them right away to capture that just-made magic.

## Summer Berry Lemonade Refresher: **Ingredients Substitutes**

![Zesty Starbucks Summer Berry Lemonade with berry splashing the drink](/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Pineapple-Spears-in-Coconut-Water-Recipe-2-1024x1024.png)

What if you're all set to make the Starbucks Summer Berry Lemonade Refresher and realize you're missing an ingredient? No need to let your spirits droop. I've got a bag full of tricks (a.k.a. substitutions) that'll get you sipping on that berry bliss in no time.

### **Substituting Lemonade:**

Got No Lemonade? Don’t break a sweat. You can quickly whisk together some freshly squeezed lemon juice, water, and a sweetener of your choice, be it sugar, honey, or even stevia for a healthier twist. The goal is to nail that balance between lip-smacking tartness and sweet serenity.

### **Mixed Berry Syrup Swaps:**

Berry Syrup Blues? If the store-bought mixed berry syrup plays hide and seek with you, it's time to put those frozen berries in your freezer to good use. Boil them down with some sugar and water. A bit of lemon zest added to the mix could also bring a nice zing to your Summer Berry Lemonade Refresher. No fresh or frozen berries? Jam or preserves can step in as a sweet, fruity stand-in. Just thin it down with a bit of water until it's pourable.

### **When Berries Are Scarce:**

Fresh Out of Berries? Let's not let that dampen the party. While fresh berries bring a burst of color and taste, you can also reach for frozen ones - they do the trick just as well. Add them in while they’re still chilly, and they'll help keep your drink cool, doubling up as ice cubes that don't dilute your beverage as they melt. Plus, as they thaw, they release their delicious flavors into the lemonade, making every sip a berry delight.

### **Ice:**

Normal Ice Too Boring? Why not jazz things up a bit? Freeze lemonade or even the mixed berry syrup in your ice tray to create ice cubes that add flavor rather than water it down as they melt. It's like your drink gets better with time, and who wouldn't love that?
