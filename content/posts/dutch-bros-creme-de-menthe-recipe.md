## **How to make Dutch Bros Creme de Menthe**

Dutch Bros Creme de Menthe is a flavor option offered by [Dutch Bros Coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Bros._Coffee), a popular coffee chain known for its various drinks and customizable options. It is a mint-flavored syrup that adds a refreshing and sweet minty flavor to beverages. You can find it in various drinks at Dutch Bros, such as espresso drinks, blended drinks, and lemonades.

### **Ingredients**

- **Coffee or Espresso:** Depending on your drink, you may need brewed coffee or a shot of espresso. Espresso is preferred for an authentic coffee shop flavor in a basic Creme de Menthe mocha.
- **Milk:** You can use any milk you prefer here, whether cow's milk or a non-dairy alternative like almond, oat, or soy milk.
- **Chocolate Sauce:** Opt for a high-quality dark or white chocolate sauce, depending on your taste preference.
- **Creme de Menthe Syrup:** This is essential for achieving that signature minty flavor. You can purchase Creme de Menthe syrup from various online retailers or specialty food stores.
- **Whipped Cream:** This is for garnishing and adding an extra layer of indulgence.
- Mint Leaves (optional) - For a fresh garnish that enhances the minty sensation.

### **Instructions**

1. **Prepare the Coffee/Espresso**: Brew your coffee or take a shot of espresso. If you're making a mocha, espresso is recommended for a stronger coffee base.
2. **Mix the Flavors**: In a saucepan over medium heat, combine one cup of milk with two tablespoons of chocolate sauce and two tablespoons of Creme de Menthe syrup. Stir continuously until the mixture is thoroughly mixed and just begins to steam. Avoid boiling to keep the milk sweet and creamy.
3. **Combine and Serve:** Pour the flavored milk mixture into a large mug over the coffee or espresso. If you're using a blender for a frozen version, blend the coffee with the milk mixture and ice until smooth. For an extra luxurious touch, top with whipped cream and drizzle a little more chocolate sauce and Creme de Menthe syrup.
4. **Garnish**: If desired, add a sprig of mint on top for a pop of color and a burst of fresh mint flavor.

### **Tips for Perfecting Your Drink**

- **Temperature Control:** Monitor the temperature when heating your milk and flavor mix. Heating the milk too much can cause it to scald, which negatively alters the flavor.
- **Quality Ingredients**: Use the highest-quality chocolate and syrups. The flavor of your drinks heavily depends on these ingredients.
- **Adjust to Taste:** The sweetness and intensity of the mint flavor can be adjusted by varying the amount of syrup and chocolate used. Start with the recommended amounts and adapt according to your preference.
- **Experiment**: Don't hesitate to experiment with different types of chocolate and milk to find your perfect combination. Some might prefer a darker, more bitter chocolate, while others might enjoy the creamy sweetness of white chocolate.

## **Ingredients Substitutes**

If you're looking to make a homemade version of Dutch Bros Creme de Menthe but need to substitute some ingredients due to allergies, dietary restrictions, or simply because you don't have them on hand, here's a list of potential substitutes for common ingredients in this type of drink:

### **For Mint Flavor**

- **Mint Extract Instead of Fresh Mint:** If you don't have fresh mint, mint extract can be a good substitute. Use it sparingly, though, as it's much more concentrated than fresh mint leaves.
- **Peppermint Oil:** Similar to mint extract, peppermint oil is very concentrated, so you'll need just a drop or two to replace a tablespoon of fresh mint.
- **Peppermint Tea:** Brew some peppermint tea and allow it to cool. It can then be used as part of the liquid base for your drink, adding a subtle mint flavor.

### **For Chocolate**

- **Cocoa Powder:** If you don't have chocolate syrup, you can dissolve cocoa powder in a bit of hot water to create a chocolate liquid. Add sweetener to adjust for the lack of sweetness in the cocoa powder.
- **Carob Powder:** If you're avoiding chocolate, carob powder can be a good alternative. Like cocoa powder, it needs to be dissolved and sweetened.
- **Chocolate Extract:** If you are out of chocolate but still want that chocolate flavor, use a few drops of chocolate extract. Be mindful, as it can be pretty potent.

### **For Cream Base**

- **Full-Fat Coconut Milk:** For those avoiding dairy or just out of cream, full-fat coconut milk can serve as a creamy, non-dairy substitute in the Creme de Menthe.
- **Almond Milk/Cashew Milk/Oat Milk**: These plant-based milk alternatives are less creamy than coconut milk but can be used as a substitute if you prefer their flavor or if they're what you have on hand. You can blend in a banana or some avocado to enhance their creaminess.
- **Half-and-Half or Milk:** If the recipe calls for heavy cream and you don't have any, you could use half-and-half or a combination of butter with milk to mimic the richness of the cream.

### **For Sweeteners**

- **Honey or Maple Syrup:** These can be natural white sugar or sweetener alternatives. The honey or maple syrup flavor will impart a different character to the drink, which some might find desirable.
- **Stevia or Monk Fruit Sweetener:** If you're looking for a sugar-free option, stevia or monk fruit sweeteners can provide sweetness without the added calories. Note that these sweeteners are more potent than sugar, so you'll need much less.

### **For Whipped Cream**

- **Coconut Whipped Cream:** You can make whipped cream from chilled coconut cream as a dairy-free alternative.
- **Store-Bought Non-Dairy Whipped Cream:** Several brands offer non-dairy whipped cream made from ingredients like soy, almond, or chickpea aquafaba, which would work well on top of a Creme de Menthe.
