## How to make Smoothie King Watermelon Lemonade

Smoothie King Watermelon Lemonade is a refreshing smoothie option that combines the flavors of watermelon and lemonade. It’s designed to be a light, [hydrating drink](/prime-hydration-drink/) with a balanced sweetness from the watermelon and tanginess from the lemonade.

### Ingredients:

#### White Grape Lemon Juice Blend:

- 1/2 cup white grape juice (for sweetness and to mimic the blend)
- 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice (adjust to your taste for the perfect tanginess)

#### Watermelon Juice Blend:

- 2 cups fresh watermelon chunks (seedless watermelon is preferred)

#### Sweetener:

- 2 tablespoons Turbinado sugar (also known as raw sugar; you can adjust this based on your preferred sweetness level or substitute with honey, agave or other sweeteners if desired)

### Instructions:

1. **Prep Your Ingredients:** Begin by preparing your watermelon, cutting it into chunks, and ensuring no seeds are present. Squeeze fresh lemons to get the required juice.
2. **Blend the Watermelon:** In a blender, add the watermelon chunks. Blend on high until you have a smooth juice consistency. You might want to strain the juice using a fine mesh to get rid of any pulp, but that's optional based on preference.
3. **Mix Your Juices:** Add the watermelon juice to the blender with the white grape juice and lemon juice. Blend these together briefly to ensure they’re well combined.
4. **Add the Sweetener:** Now, introduce the Turbinado sugar to the blender. Blend again until the sugar is completely dissolved. Taste and adjust the lemon juice or sugar according to your liking.
5. **Chill:** For the best flavor, chill the mixture in the refrigerator for about an hour or serve over ice immediately for a refreshing drink.
6. **Serve**: Pour into your favorite glass, garnish with a slice of lemon or watermelon if desired, and enjoy!

### Tips for Perfecting Your Smoothie King Watermelon Lemonade:

- **Select Ripe Watermelon:** For a sweeter, more flavorful smoothie, ensure your watermelon is ripe. A good indicator is a watermelon that feels heavy for its size and has a creamy yellow spot where it rests on the ground.
- **Adjust Sweetness and Tartness**: The beauty of making this drink at home is the ability to customize it. Start with less lemon juice and sweetener, then adjust to taste. Remember, you can always add more but can't take it away.
- **Chill Your Ingredients:** For an extra refreshing smoothie, chill your watermelon and white grape juice before blending.
- **Make It a Smoothie:** If you prefer a more traditional smoothie consistency, add a cup of ice before blending.
- **Sweetener Substitutions:** If Turbinado sugar isn't available, feel free to use other sweeteners. Liquid sweeteners like honey or agave nectar mix particularly well.

### What to serve with

The Smoothie King Watermelon Lemonade is a refreshing, hydrating treat perfect for warm weather. Its vibrant flavor and cooling effect make it an excellent stand-alone beverage, but it can also wonderfully complement a range of dishes and snacks. When thinking about what to serve with this delightful drink, consider the setting—whether it’s a casual gathering, a summertime party, or a relaxing day at home.

#### 1. Light Appetizers:

- **Fruit Skewers:** Simple and refreshing, fruit skewers can include a variety of seasonal fruits like pineapple, strawberries, and grapes. They match the sweetness and freshness of the lemonade.
- **[Caprese Skewers](https://www.loveandlemons.com/caprese-skewers/):** A savory option, Caprese skewers with cherry tomatoes, mozzarella balls, and basil leaves drizzled with balsamic glaze are a delightful complement to the sweet and tangy lemonade.

#### 2. Main Dishes:

- **Grilled Chicken or Fish:** Opt for lightly seasoned grilled chicken or white fish like tilapia or halibut. The light, savory flavors of the grill marks pair wonderfully with the refreshing qualities of the lemonade.
- **Salads:** A crisp, green salad with components like mixed greens, cucumbers, avocados, and a light vinaigrette dressing complements the lemonade without overpowering it.

#### 3. Sides:

- **Couscous or Quinoa Salad**: These light, fluffy grains mixed with vegetables like chopped bell peppers, cucumber, and cherry tomatoes, with a lemon herb dressing, resonate with the brightness of the Watermelon Lemonade.
- **Veggie Platter with Hummus:** A colorful arrangement of raw vegetables such as carrots, celery, bell peppers, and broccoli served with a creamy hummus dip provides a healthy, crunch-filled snack that pairs excellently with the drink.

#### 4. Desserts:

- **Sorbet**: Lemon or raspberry sorbet shares similar refreshing qualities to lemonade, making it a perfect sweet treat to enjoy alongside.
- **[Angel Food Cake](https://www.browneyedbaker.com/angel-food-cake/):** Light and airy, this cake won't weigh you down and complements the fruity notes of the lemonade without being overly sweet.

#### 5. For Casual Gatherings:

- **Cheese and Crackers:** A selection of mild cheeses and an assortment of crackers can be a great snack to nibble on while enjoying your drink.
- **Bruschetta:** The tangy and savory flavors of tomato, basil, olive oil, and garlic atop crusty bread make Bruschetta a fabulous appetizer to serve with the refreshing drink.

## Ingredients Substitutes

![Watermelon Lemonade Smoothie King](/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Add-a-heading-10-1024x1024.png)

Creating a homemade version of Smoothie King’s Watermelon Lemonade can be both fun and satisfying. However, if you don’t have all the exact ingredients on hand or wish to make some adjustments to suit dietary preferences or enhance nutritional content, consider using substitutes.

### Watermelon

**Primary Ingredient:** Watermelon is crucial for its juicy, sweet flavor.

**Substitutes:** If watermelon isn’t available or if you’re looking for a different taste profile, you can try other melons like cantaloupe or honeydew. These will alter the flavor but still provide a similar sweetness and hydrating effect.

### Lemon Juice

**Primary Ingredient:** Fresh lemon juice provides tartness and brightness, balancing the sweetness of the watermelon.

**Substitutes:** Lime juice can be a direct substitute, offering a slightly different citrus note. Apple cider vinegar is another alternative, although more potent, so use it sparingly to avoid overpowering the drink.

### Sweetener (e.g., Honey, Sugar)

**Primary Ingredient:** Typically, some form of sweetener is added to enhance the watermelon's natural sweetness.

#### Substitutes:

- **Agave syrup:** A natural sweetener that is sweeter than sugar, use less quantity.
- **Stevia or monk fruit sweetener:** For a sugar-free version, these are great options. They are much sweeter than sugar, so adjust according to taste.
- **Maple syrup:** This can provide a unique flavor twist besides sweetening the drink.

## Final Thoughts

The light, refreshing Smoothie King Watermelon Lemonade pairs best with foods that are not too heavy or overpowering, allowing both your meal and the drink to shine. Enjoy experimenting with these suggestions to find your perfect summer pairing!
